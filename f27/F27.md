## The question

For a map P: F_3^k → F_3^k and X uniform on F_3^k, let c_t(v) = #{x : P(x) + t·x = v} and

    S(P) = Σ_{t ∈ F_3} Σ_v c_t(v) log2 c_t(v),     h(P) = k log2 3 − S(P) / 3^(k+1).

With h_k = min_P h(P), the Kakeya growth base over F_3 is β_3 = inf_k 2^(h_k/k)
(Theorem 2 of this repository's README; not checked by a human). The best one-dimensional
map (0, 1, 2 ↦ 0, 0, 1) gives h_1 = log2 3 − 2/3 and β_3 ≤ 3/2^(2/3) = 1.88988, the bound of
Kopparty, Lev, Saraf and Sudan ([arXiv:1003.3736](https://arxiv.org/abs/1003.3736)), open
since 2010 (best lower bound 1.8). At block length k = 3 its coordinatewise cube has
S = 162 exactly, so **a single map P of F_3^3 with S(P) > 162 would give β_3 < 1.88988.**

Already known here: h_2 = 2 h_1 (all 9^9 maps of F_3^2, `exact_h2.py`), and every quadratic
map of F_3^3 has S ≤ 162 (`notes/a192.md`, a C scan of 15 × 3^18 maps). A map that beats 162
must therefore have algebraic degree at least 3 and respect no proper subspace.

## What this folder scans

We identify F_3^3 with F_27 = F_3[a]/(a^3 + 2a + 1) (the Conway polynomial), so vector
addition is field addition and t·x (t ∈ F_3) is scalar multiplication. The power map x^e has
algebraic degree equal to the base-3 digit sum of e, so most F_27-polynomials are not
quadratic: 17 of the 26 exponents 1..26 have digit sum at least 3, up to 6 for x^26.

* `test_f27_trinomials.py` / `TRINOMIALS.md`: every P = c1 x^e1 + c2 x^e2 + c3 x^e3 with at
  most three terms.
* `test_f27_powerlinear.py` / `POWERLINEAR.md`: every L(x^e) and every c x^e + L(x), with L
  any of the 3^9 F_3-linear maps of F_27.

## Symmetries (proofs)

1. **Constants.** P + b has the counts of P shifted by b.
2. **Role shifts.** P + s·x (s ∈ F_3) permutes the three values of t.
3. **Linear conjugation.** For an invertible F_3-linear B, P' = B⁻¹ ∘ P ∘ B satisfies
   P'(x) + t·x = B⁻¹(P(Bx) + t·Bx); as Bx runs over F_3^3, the counts of P' are those of P.
   For B = multiplication by μ ∈ F_27^*, c x^e becomes c μ^(e−1) x^e. So the first
   coefficient whose exponent is not 1 can be moved into a fixed set of representatives of
   the cosets of {μ^(e−1)} (there are gcd(e − 1, 26) of them), while every other coefficient
   runs over all of F_27^*. For c x^e + L the linear part becomes μ⁻¹ L μ, which again runs
   over all linear maps.

## Controls (`test_f27_controls.py`)

* Field axioms on all 27^3 triples; a generator of order 26; a^3 + 2a + 1 = 0; x^3 additive.
* The table-driven score against an independent implementation from coordinate tuples (300
  random maps) and against the exact integer log2 ∏ c^c.
* The coordinatewise product map scores exactly 162 (∏ c^c = 2^162).
* All three symmetries on random maps; the coset representatives cover every coefficient.
* Every one of the 3^9 linear maps scores at most 81 log2 3 = 128.38 (the sum over t of
  27 · dim ker(L + t) · log2 3), attained.

Every score within 10⁻⁶ of 162 is decided exactly by comparing the integer ∏ c^c with 2^162.

## Limits

The families are small slices of the 27^27 maps of F_3^3; a negative answer on them says
nothing about general cubic maps. Theorem 2 and the reductions here are not checked by a
human mathematician.
