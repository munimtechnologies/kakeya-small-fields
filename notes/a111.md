  each tree must weigh 1 in total (2^-depth each), which is how the job checks that it covered every
  assignment (cube_groups/m124.py, m124-predictions.md).

## Limits

- The 344 refutations without certificates are solver answers until they are checked. "UNKNOWN"
  says nothing about a case, and a cube's UNSAT has no certificate until a per-cube DRAT run.
- A certificate certifies the CNF only. Everything rests on hand lemmas no human has checked
  (Lemmas 102 to 106) and on the encoders m42, m100, m101 and m102.
