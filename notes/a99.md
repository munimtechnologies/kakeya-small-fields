## What changes

- **No anisotropy at all (m106e):** in each of the six core cases and each of the four k = 6
  cases, the CNF with every hand condition of the case but **no** clause sigma(h) != 0 is already
  UNSAT (6 to 56 s). The positive control, with the same conditions and k = 10, is SAT (0.4 s). So
  for these ten commuting spaces T(N), the system (*), the normal form and the hand conditions
  contradict each other on their own.
- **d = 2:** 24 of the 28 cases left open by jobs m105a-d are UNSAT (22 with anisotropy only on N,
  24 with full anisotropy at 120 s). So 1,111 of the 1,115 d = 2 cases with k(T(N)) <= 4 are UNSAT
  (uncertified), and in ten of the thirteen d = 2 classes every such case is. The four left are
  t2 cases of type nil3.
- **d = 1 and d = 0 (m106b):** anisotropy only on N decides 33 of the 37 k <= 4 cases of the d = 1
  class o007_c008, including all 31 that full anisotropy decided in job m103b and two it did not.
  In the two d = 0 classes it decides only 5 of 36, and 6 cases are SAT there: anisotropy on N is
  not enough at d = 0.
- **Every graded check held** (7 in m106a, 15 in m106b, 10 in m106e).
- **Job m107 (queued):** which of the twelve groups of hand conditions the ten refutations need,
  no anisotropy on all 88 cases with k > 4 of the three d = 3 classes, and the four open d = 2 cases

Because "none" was UNSAT, the searches for minimal sets of vectors had nothing to do: the minimal
set inside N and inside span(h_5, h_6, h_7) is empty in all ten cases.

**Observation (uncertified).** For each of these ten commuting spaces T(N), there is no system
satisfying (*) on V = F_2^11, the normal form and the hand conditions, even without asking for
sigma(h) != 0 at any h. The caution of [a98.md](a98.md) applies with more force: the hand
conditions were derived by hand from the anisotropy of the whole system (they are proved, by hand,
unchecked by a human), so this does not show that some T(N) is impossible without anisotropy. It
shows that a proof for these ten cases needs no anisotropy beyond what Lemmas 103 to 106 and the
hints already extract, which makes a hand proof a much smaller target. It also suggests that
"none" may decide cases with k >= 6, which full anisotropy mostly could not (job m102: one k >= 8
case of four at 300 s); job m107b/c tests that on all 88 such cases of the d = 3 classes.

## The decision rules

- **m106a, not all decided:** the four open cases are reported above by type (t2, nil3), and their
  three classes are not certified yet. Job m107a gives them 900 s each at "n". If all four are
  UNSAT, DRAT certificates of the 1,115 follow (job m108, at most four shards per run, each from
  the smallest CNF that was UNSAT for its case) toward Proposition 39 (in the rank-0 case of
  (B7)_8, d >= 2 implies k(T(N)) >= 6).
- **m106b:** "n" decided more than full anisotropy did (38 against 34), and the median time per case
  was 11.7 s, so by the rule "n" is used for the cases with k <= 4 at d <= 1. The pooled median
  hides that "n" fails at d = 0 (median at the cap, 6 SAT), so it is used for the 3,876 d = 1 cases
  only, not for the 1,500 d = 0 cases. That run (about 14 job-hours, five shards under 3 hours) is
  planned for the next run, after job m107's five jobs, so as not to fill the shared queue.
- **m106e, "none" UNSAT for every core case:** the hand conditions alone rule these cases out;
  that is the target of a hand proof (Lemma 110). Before trying it, job m107e/f asks which of the
  twelve groups of hand conditions each refutation needs (a deletion-minimal set of groups), so
  the proof has a precise list of hypotheses. "none" is run next on the cases with k > 4, where it
  is most needed (job m107b/c), rather than on the k <= 4 cases, which are already decided.
- **k = 6 UNSAT at "none":** the structure is not special to k <= 4. That is why m107b/c runs "none"
  on all 88 cases with k = 6, 8 and 10 of the three d = 3 classes.
