## Both proof checks were stopped from outside

In each job one case was UNSAT in CaDiCaL but is recorded as DRAT_STOPPED ("time limit"):

- r3b_t8_B7_o012_c004_t2_m154: CaDiCaL 445 s, then drat-trim for **11,605 s**
- r3b_t8_B7_o012_c000_t3_m103_188: CaDiCaL 725 s, then drat-trim for **11,151 s**

m104.run_drat gives drat-trim its own limit (-t 4000), and the Python parent kills it after
4,060 s of wall-clock time. That can overshoot by about 7,000 s only if the whole container was not
running. The two checks started about 8 minutes apart (near 04:01 and 04:09 UTC), and both normal
cut-offs would have fired before 05:20. Yet both "time limit" records closed within a minute of
each other, near 07:15 UTC, and both jobs finished at 07:18:34 and 07:18:35. The natural reading
is that both containers were paused together, from before 05:09 until about 07:14 UTC, and their
timers fired on resume. The job logs do not say what paused them. Because m134 starts no new case after 12,000 s
of the cert step, the remaining cases never started.

Nothing about the satisfiability of the two stopped cases follows from this beyond CaDiCaL's
unverified UNSAT. They stay uncertified until a proof is checked.
