## What ran

After a128 (all six pilot cases of the d = 0 block certified), 1,494 of its 1,500 cases had no
certificate. Before a census, m132 ran a stratified sample of 120 of them: the 1,494 names sorted,
index floor(k * 1494 / 120) for k = 0 .. 119, in three shards of 40 (k mod 3). Each case ran alone
at the **sweep** CNF (anisotropy on all of V) with m113.certify: CaDiCaL 1,500 s with an ASCII DRAT
proof, drat-trim 4,000 s; no new case after 25,000 s; 1 CPU per shard.
