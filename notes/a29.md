## Corollary 53 (no (A5) for t = 7)

**Corollary 53.** Let an anisotropic system with t = 7 and dim V = 9 have no element of rank 4. Then
no element of rank 3 is in the configuration (A5) of Proposition 29(c).

*Proof.* Let u have rank 3 and be in (A5). Lemma 79 (a) ([a24.md](a24.md)) and Lemma 82 (a26.md) give
bases in which x0 = e_0, R = span(e_1, e_2, e_3), P = span(e_0, ..., e_5), phi(v_1) = phi(v_2) = 0,
phi(v_(3+j)) = e_j and A_u is the matrix of Lemma 82. So the plain CNF of job m41 (the 112 unit
clauses of that form on sat_aniso.encode for (7, 9)) is satisfiable. Job m41a found it
unsatisfiable. QED

What it rests on: Proposition 29(c) and Lemma 79 (a) (by hand, a24.md, which use A(s) = s for s <= 6
through Theorem 31); Lemma 82 (by hand, and checked by the orbit closure of the selfcheck); and one
solver answer of m41a, saved without a proof certificate. The controls checked the encoder (the
t = 5 CNFs are exactly those of case_a.py) and that a known system is found (the planted system at
t = 6). The hint clauses of m41b are not used.

## Lemma 85 (shape (c) at rank 4: the orbits of R)

Setting as in a26.md, Lemma 81, at t = 7 and dim V = 9: u of rank 4 in Case B, shape (c), with the
form of Lemma 37 of [case-b-shapes.md](case-b-shapes.md):

    x0 = e_0,  P = span(e_1, ..., e_6),  W = ker A_u = span(e_0, e_1, e_2),  N = span(u, v_1, v_2),
    sigma(v_1) = e_1,  sigma(v_2) = e_2,  beta(v_1, v_2) = 0,  A_(v_1), A_(v_2) kill W,
    phi(v_(2+j)) = e_j for 1 <= j <= 6.

Write Y = span(e_1, e_2) = W n P, R = Im A_u (dimension 4), and

    K = {g in GL(X) : g e_0 = e_0, g W = W, g P = P}.

Every g in K keeps the form. Replace the system by g sigma, g beta, g A_h g^(-1). Then g Y = Y, and
since beta = 0 on N, sigma is linear on span(v_1, v_2) and maps it onto g Y = Y, so there is exactly
one basis v'_1, v'_2 of span(v_1, v_2) with new values sigma(v'_i) = e_i. beta(v'_1, v'_2) = 0, and
the new A_(v'_i) kill g W = W. The new v_(2+j) are chosen over e_j, which lies in g P = P. This
is Lemma 39 of shape-c.md, extended by the maps that add to each e_j (j >= 3) a vector of Y. So R can be
replaced by any g R, and then A_u by its conjugate under any element of K that keeps R. By
Corollary 51, dim(R n W) >= 2.

**Lemma 85.** The K-orbits of 4-dimensional subspaces R of X with dim(R n W) >= 2 number 5:

    type   R n W                                        orbit size
    W      W  (R contains W)                            15
    YP     Y, and R lies in P                           35
    Yn     Y, and R does not lie in P                   105
    X      one of the 3 planes of W through e_0         420
    G      one of the 3 planes of W that are neither    420
           Y nor through e_0

The sizes add up to 995, the number of 4-dimensional subspaces of F_2^7 that meet a given
3-dimensional subspace in dimension at least 2.

*Proof.* *Step 1 (K in blocks).* g P = P and g W = W give g Y = Y, and g is determined by g e_0 = e_0
and its restriction to P. In the basis e_1, e_2 | e_3, ..., e_6 of P that restriction is

    [ b  M ]
    [ 0  A ]      with b in GL_2(F_2), A in GL_4(F_2) and M any 2 x 4 matrix,

and every such matrix gives an element of K. So |K| = 6 x 2^8 x 20160 = 30965760. (The same order
comes from the stabiliser of the plane Y in GL(P), which is what this block form describes.)

*Step 2 (the invariant R n W).* As in Lemma 84 (a28.md), write R_W = R n W, r = dim R_W in {2, 3},
Rbar for the image of R in X / W = F_2^4 (identified through e_3, ..., e_6), of dimension m = 4 - r,
and f : Rbar -> W / R_W for the map with R = {w + x + f(x)}. Elements of K map W to itself, so they
map R_W to (g R)_W. The group acts on W by fixing e_0 and acting on Y by b, so the 7 planes of W fall
into the orbits {Y}, the 3 planes through e_0, and the 3 others (Lemma 81), and W itself is fixed.

*Step 3 (orbits for a fixed R_W).* Fix R_W. The elements with b = I and M = 0 act on Rbar through A,
and GL_4 is transitive on m-dimensional subspaces, with the stabiliser of Rbar inducing all of
GL(Rbar). The elements with b = I and A = I send f to f + M|Rbar modulo R_W, where M is any linear map
from X / W into Y. So once Rbar is fixed, what is left of f is its composite with
W / R_W -> W / (R_W + Y).
- If r = 3, W / R_W = 0 and there is one orbit, of size [4 choose 1]_2 = 15.
- If R_W is a plane other than Y, then R_W + Y = W and there is one orbit for each such R_W, of size
  [4 choose 2]_2 x 2^2 = 35 x 4 = 140. Elements with M = 0 and A = I move R_W through b, so the 3
  planes through e_0 give one orbit of size 420, and so do the 3 others.
- If R_W = Y, W / Y is a line, the composite is a linear form on Rbar, and A acts on it by composing
  with an element of GL(Rbar). So there are two orbits: the form is 0, which means R lies in
  Y + span(e_3, ..., e_6) = P (35 subspaces, f = 0), or it is not (35 x 3 = 105).
g P = P, so "R lies in P" is kept by K, and the five orbits are distinct. QED

**The same count at t = 5, 6.** With A_u of rank t - 3, |K| = 6 x 2^(2(t - 3)) x |GL_(t-3)(F_2)| =
576 and 64512, and the orbit sizes are

    t = 6:  W 1, YP 7, Yn 7, X 42, G 42      (total 99)
    t = 5:  YP 1, X 3, G 3                   (total 7; R is a plane of W)

At t = 4 no subspace R of dimension 1 meets W in dimension 2, as A(4) = 4 requires.

**Classes of A_u when R contains W.** The stabiliser of R = W + span(e_3) acts on the 20160
isomorphisms L : X / W -> R by L -> g|_R L A^(-1), with g|_R and A independent: g|_R fixes e_0, acts
on Y by b and sends e_3 to e_3 + y with y in Y (24 maps), and A runs over the stabiliser of [e_3] in
GL_4. So the classes correspond to the orbits of the 24 maps on the non-zero vectors of R, namely the
value L[e_3]. There are 5: e_0; the 3 non-zero vectors of Y; e_0 plus one of them; e_3 + Y; and
e_0 + e_3 + Y. At t = 6 (R = W) there is one class. The selfcheck of m44.py compares both counts with
the machine.

## Job m44 (queued, not run)

`cube_groups/m44.py` uses case_b.py and case_b_shapes.py unchanged. For each orbit representative R
it computes the classes of A_u (the 20160 matrices with kernel W and image R) under conjugation by
24 random units of the algebra {g : g e_0 in span(e_0), g W <= W, g Y <= Y, g P <= P, g R <= R},
whose units are exactly the stabiliser of R in K. Every unit is checked to lie in it, so every
computed class lies inside one true class and the split is complete either way. At t = 5 and 6 the
computed classes must equal the exact orbits of the whole stabiliser, found by listing K.

Each instance is the CNF of case_b_shapes.encode_shape(7, 3, 'c') with 28 unit clauses that fix
A_u on e_3, ..., e_6, plus new variables for the 4 x 4 matrices M_1, M_2 of M_(v_1), M_(v_2) on
X / W (Lemma 38 of shape-c.md, in the basis [e_3], ..., [e_6]) and these conditions:

- (S) A_(v_i) e_(3+j) = A_u (sum over l of M_i[l][j] e_(3+l)): the definition of M_i, Lemma 38;
- Lemma 38 (a), [beta(v_i, h)] = M_i [phi(h)]: rows 3 to 6 of beta(v_i, v_(2+j)) are 0 for j = 1, 2
  and column j - 3 of M_i for j >= 3;
- Lemma 38 (b): M_1 M_2 = M_2 M_1;
- Corollary 51: M_n != 0 for every n != 0 in N, with M_u = I (6 clauses).

These are 120 linear conditions and 6 clauses. Lemma 38 is proved by hand from (*) and (Q), which
the CNF already encodes, so (S), (a) and (b) state consequences of the CNF explicitly. Corollary 51
uses A(6) <= 7.

The controls: case_b's (t, k) = (2, 1) example must be SAT, and every class at t = 5 and 6
(dim V = t + 2) must be UNSAT, as A(t) = t there. There is no positive control in form c: a
satisfiable one at t <= 6 would contradict A(t) = t. Instead, the unit tests check the conditions
on hand-made data at t = 6. They hold for (M_1, M_2) = (N, N^2) with N a regular nilpotent matrix,
and they fail for (N, N), for a pair that does not commute, and for an A_(v_1) that does not match
M_1.

    m44a   unit tests; selfcheck; controls (20 minutes); part 0 of 4 of the classes, 100 minutes
    m44b   part 1 of 4, 145 minutes
    m44c   part 2 of 4, 145 minutes
    m44d   part 3 of 4, 145 minutes

Within a job each class gets an equal share of the time left, at least 20 seconds and at most 25
minutes. The predictions and the decision rule were written before the jobs were queued
([cube_groups/m44-predictions.md](../cube_groups/m44-predictions.md)).

What a result would mean. If every class is UNSAT, shape (c) does not occur at rank 4 for t = 7 and
dim V = 9 (computer-assisted, resting on Lemmas 35, 37, 38 and 85 and Corollary 51). With Corollary
52 (Case A), Lemma 42 (shape (d1)) and shape (d2) (job m43), no element would have rank 4, and then
Corollary 53 applies. A verified SAT would give A(7) = 9.

## What does not follow

- A(7) <= 8 is still open: 7 <= A(7) <= 9.
- Corollary 53 needs the hypothesis that there is no element of rank 4. That hypothesis is the
  first four rows of the table, two of which are still open.
- Lemma 85 counts orbits of R. It does not count the classes of A_u (except when R contains W), which
  m44 computes by machine. If the random units missed part of a stabiliser, there are only more
  classes, not fewer.
