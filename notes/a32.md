## What the timing says

At t = 6 the analogous configuration ((B5) there) needed 10 to 24 minutes for some classes (jobs
m33, m34), and its last four took 709 to 1435 s (jobs m36, m37). At t = 7 the CNF has 48,587
variables and about 286,000 clauses. The four classes that closed did so at 70 to 95 percent of
their budget, so the open ones may well need several times more. Jobs m42c and m42d give each of the 420 classes of (B5) about 40 s, so most of
them will probably stay open too.

Counting every class that A(7) <= 8 still needs by this method: 64 of (B6) (30 open plus the 34 of
m42b), 420 of (B5), 210 of shape (d2) (Lemma 87) and 54 of shape (c) (Lemma 86), 748 in all. At 10
to 30 minutes each that is about 125 to 375 CPU hours, a lot for one CPU per job on a shared queue.
So the search alone is a long road. Hand facts that rule out whole pair orbits, or that add linear
conditions the solver cannot find by itself, are worth more than longer budgets. (The conditions
m42 adds for ker phi are instances of (*) and (Q) involving u, written as linear equations once
u's data is fixed. The same can be written for the other six basis vectors, from (1), (2) and (*)
at h' = u of [a10.md](a10.md). Whether such restatements speed the solver up has not been
measured.)

## Job m45 (queued)

`cube_groups/m45.py` runs the instances of m42.py unchanged (it imports a copy of m42.py). An
instance is defined by the representative A_u alone, so m45 lists the 30 representatives that m42a
recorded and solves them directly. Its first step recomputes m42.py's classification of (B6) with
the same code and seeds and checks that it has the same 68 classes, the same per-pair counts, and
the same 30 representatives, each with kernel X_1 and image R of its pair. m45a takes the 15 open
classes of pair orbits 9, 10 and 12, and m45b the other 15, each in 156 minutes with at most 11 per
class.

If a class is still open after m45, the rule written before it ran is not to escalate blindly but
first to write down how long the solved ones took and whether a pass at about 30 minutes per class
fits the queue, and otherwise to split the class further by hand.
