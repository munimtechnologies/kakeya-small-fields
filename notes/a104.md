MV2 of Munim, Inc., 2026-10-26. Added to kakeya-small-fields next to [a103.md](a103.md). It was
researched and written with AI agents working in MV2's name. Jobs m111a-b7-struct-v1,

m110i has not come back. **No class is closed, and A(8) <= 11 is still the best bound proved.** The

## What changes

- **The observation of [a103.md](a103.md) is machine-checked.** Job m111a recomputed pi(R), the
  map S that A_u induces on Q, and, for each of the 3,876 d = 1 cases with k(T(N)) <= 4, whether
  T(N) leaves pi(R) invariant, from A_u and the spaces themselves. The reading of the names was
  exact: in shards a to g of m110, 313 of the 315 open cases leave pi(R) invariant. Only 486 of the
  3,876 cases (12.5%) do, and of the 326 such cases in shards a to g, 313 (96%) stayed open, against
  2 of the 2,389 others (0.08%).
- **Shards h and j:** 675 more cases UNSAT, 99 open; read off the names with the rule m111a
  checked, 98 of the 99 open cases leave pi(R) invariant (the exception is o010_c008 t3_m063_205).
  Over the nine shards back, 3,075 of 3,489 cases are UNSAT (uncertified) and 414 open, 411 of them
  pi(R)-invariant.
- **Partial anisotropy does not decide them; full anisotropy mostly does.** On one open case per
  label (22 labels), anisotropy on N + span(h_1..h_4) (255 vectors) decided nothing at 120 s, and
  on N + span(h_5, h_6, h_7) (127 vectors) only the case that does not leave pi(R) invariant
  (o008_c003 t2_m092, UNSAT in 111 s); two cases even have verified models there. The full sweep
  CNF at 600 s refuted 14 of the other 21, in 186 to 582 s. One of them, o007_c000 t3_m031_036, is
  SAT on the 127 vectors and UNSAT on all of V, so its refutation needs vectors outside N +
  span(h_5, h_6, h_7).
- **Job m112 (queued, twelve shards):** the sweep CNF at 1,500 s on the 399 cases still open.
- Every graded check held in all five jobs.

## The decision rules

- **Struct confirms the reading:** done; recorded above as a computer-checked count, not a theorem.
  The hand reason sketched in [a103.md](a103.md) (when T(N) leaves pi(R) invariant, each A_n maps
  R + X_1 into A_u(R)) is still only a heuristic.
- **"in4" or "out3" decides at least 80%:** did not fire.
- **Neither level decides half, the 600 s sweep decides most:** fired (14 of 21). The rule asked
  for the sweep at 600 s on the open pi(R)-invariant cases. Because the pilot's UNSAT answers sat
  close to 600 s, job m112 uses 1,500 s, and it also takes the two open cases that are not
  pi(R)-invariant.

## Limits

- The d = 1 answers are solver answers without certificates; "UNKNOWN" says nothing about a case.
- The pi(R) count is computer-checked for shards a to g; for h and j it is read off the names with
  the rule m111a checked, and m112's check part recomputes it. It is a count, not a theorem.
- Everything rests on hand lemmas no human has checked (Lemmas 102 to 106) and on the encoders m42,
  m100, m101 and m102.
