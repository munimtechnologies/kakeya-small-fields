## Job cert1: a first pilot of proof certificates

Every UNSAT answer in these notes is a solver answer saved without a proof certificate; that has
been the first item under Limits in every note. Job cert1 (cube_groups/cert1.py, predictions in
cube_groups/cert1-predictions.md, written before it ran) is a first step past that. It rebuilds 99
of o005_c003's 1961 split instances (every 20th in m66's order, three of them among the hard 32
above) with the same code that ran them, solves each with a standalone CaDiCaL (rel-2.2.1, built
from source) that writes a DRAT proof, and checks every proof with drat-trim (M. Heule et al.),
an independent checker. Its own controls: the pigeonhole formula PHP(6, 5) must give a proof that
drat-trim accepts, the same formula with an empty proof must be rejected, and PHP(5, 5) must come
back SAT with a model that satisfies it.

A VERIFIED answer certifies only that the CNF has no solution. It does not certify the encoding,
the hints, Lemma 93 or Proposition 33. If the pilot verifies all 99 cheaply, the rule written down
before it ran is to certify the whole class, then the other classes of (B6) in turn.
