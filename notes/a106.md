## The decision rules

- **A DRAT run stops at its cap** (m113): re-run alone with the caps doubled. Job m115 does this for
  o007_c001 t2_m026 and o007_c009 t3_m011_017 (CaDiCaL 1,200 s, drat-trim 2,400 s), and checks
  that each CNF has the SHA-256 m113 recorded. Predictions in cube_groups/m115-predictions.md.
- **Cases that have not had the 1,500 s sweep:** job m116 runs it on the 14 that m112i-k skipped
  (two shards of 7). Predictions in cube_groups/m116-predictions.md.
- **UNKNOWN at 1,500 s:** no longer cap. Cube and conquer waits for m112l and m116, so that every
  UNKNOWN case is split in one job. Since the hard cases now carry eight labels, a hand lemma for
  three labels would no longer be enough.
