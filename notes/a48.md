## Job m59

Code: cube_groups/m59.py, which imports m57.py and m55.py unchanged. Predictions and rules:
cube_groups/m59-predictions.md, written before either group ran.

- **Group a (m59a), (B6):** a117 and a153 under CaDiCaL for 15 minutes, then the XOR arm for 15
  minutes if still open (m58's rule for 1 to 10 open). Then the kind-L class as its 308 split
  instances, CaDiCaL, at most 25 s each.
- **Group b (m59b), (B5):** o009_c009 and o016_c024 under the XOR arm, 5 minutes each (m57's
  rule). Then the splits of o002_c000 (421 instances) and o001_c000 (445), CaDiCaL, at most 20 s
  each.

**Kind L is counted again, in m42's coordinates.** Job m52 matched Proposition 31 (308 classes,
51 / 174 / 83 by rank on X_1) in a frame of its own, with x0 = e_6. Rather than move its
representatives across, m59 counts the classes again directly in m42's coordinates (x0 = e_0,
P = span(e_1, ..., e_6), A_u = e_4 -> e_2, e_5 -> e_1, e_6 -> e_0). It solves for the stabiliser G
of (x0, P, A_u) from its linear conditions, takes the maps that kill x0, have image in R and rank at
most 2 on X_1 (other than 0 and A_u), and joins them by union-find under generators of G and
A -> A + A_u. The job solves nothing unless this second count gives m52's |G| = 12288, |Adm| =
176126 (= 344 * 512 - 2 by hand) and 308 = 51 / 174 / 83. If it does, Proposition 31 has been
matched twice, by two programs in two frames.

The (B5) splits use m55.py's own representatives, recomputed in the job. Its union-find keeps the
smallest index of each class as the root, so the representatives do not depend on the random
generators. Each one is checked again in m57's terms against Proposition 32 (a), including
rank(A_(h_1) + A_u) <= 3.

Guesses (low confidence): a117 and a153 both UNSAT; at least 1 of the 2 XOR classes; kind L 290
to 308 of 308; the (B5) splits 400 to 421 and 420 to 445.

Rules: if a117 and a153 both close, the kind-graph class is empty, and (B6) goes to 16 of 68. If
all 308 kind-L instances close, that class is empty too. If a (B5) split closes every instance,
that class is empty. Instances still open get 5 minutes in each arm (more than 30 open: a 60 s pass
first); a rank-0 instance open after both arms gets the third-matrix split by hand first.
