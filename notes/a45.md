## Proposition 32

Coordinates are m42.py's: x0 = sigma(u) = e_0, P = phi(V) = span(e_1, ..., e_5),
H0 = P + span(x0) = span(e_0, ..., e_5), and e_6 lies outside H0. u is in (B5) with d = 3:
X_1 = ker A_u has dimension 4 and contains x0 and R = Im A_u. N = ker phi has dimension 4, and the
basis of V of the normal form puts u and three elements of N first, so A_(h_1) for h_1 = v_1 can be
any A_n with n in N outside span(u).

**Proposition 32.** Let u be in (B5) with d = 3.

(a) For every n in N outside span(u), A = A_n lies in the set **Adm** of maps A with A x0 = 0,
    A(H0) inside R, rank of A on X_1 at most 2, rank A <= 3, rank (A + A_u) <= 3, A != 0 and
    A != A_u.

(b) |Adm| = 405566 when X_1 lies in H0 (pair orbits 0, 1, 2) and 323966 when it does not (pair
    orbits 20, 31, 32, 33), whatever the class of A_u.

(c) The group G of g in GL(X) with g x0 = x0, g P = P and g A_u = A_u g has the orders below, for
    the representatives that jobs m42c and m42d recorded.

(d) Up to G and A -> A + A_u, Adm has at least ceil(|Adm| / (2 |G|)) classes:

        class        X_1 in H0   R                       |G|     at least   status
        o000_c000    yes         e_0, e_1, e_2           24576       9      open (m46)
        o000_c001    yes         e_0, e_1, e_2            8192      25      open (m48a)
        o000_c002    yes         e_0, e_1, e_2            8192      25      in m48b
        o001_c000    yes         e_1, e_2, e_3           24576       9      in m48c (staged)
        o002_c000    yes         e_3, e_2, e_0 + e_1     24576       9      open (m46)
        o002_c001    yes         e_3, e_2, e_0 + e_1      4096      50      open (m46)
        o020_c000    no          e_0, e_1, e_2            3072      53      open (m46)
        o031_c000    no          e_6, e_1, e_0             512     317      UNSAT (m48a)
        o032_c000    no          e_6, e_2, e_1            1536     106      open (m46)
        o033_c000    no          e_6, e_2, e_0 + e_1       512     317      in m48b

    For the nine classes not yet UNSAT that is at least 603 instances.

*Proof.* (a) As in Proposition 30 (a): A_n x0 = 0 and A_n(H0) inside R by Proposition 29 (c);
A_n kills x0 and sigma(n), independent vectors of X_1 ((N) of a36.md and Lemma 30 (b)), so its rank
on X_1 is at most 2; A_n is not 0 or A_u by Corollary 60 (d = 3 >= 2). Every element has rank at
most 3 (Corollary 55), and n + u is in N outside span(u) with A_(n + u) = A_n + A_u. In (B6) the last
two rank conditions were automatic, as the image lay in R; here A e_6 is free.

(b) Write v = A e_6.

*X_1 inside H0.* X_1 = span(x0) + C with C = span(e_1, e_2, e_3), and H0 = X_1 + D with
D = span(e_4, e_5). A is given by K = A on C and L = A on D (maps into R, 3 x 3 and 3 x 2) and v.
A_u is 0 on C, maps D onto a plane R_P of R (call this N = A_u on D) and sends e_6 into R. Rank on
X_1 at most 2 means K singular. If v is in R, both images lie in R and the rank conditions hold:
8 * 344 * 64 = 176128 maps. If v is not in R (120 choices), they read rank [K | L] <= 2 and
rank [K | L + N] <= 2. Count the pairs (K, L) by rank K. Rank 2: the column space U of K must
contain the columns of L and of L + N, so U = R_P; 42 matrices K have column space exactly a
given plane, and L has 16 choices in R_P: 672. Rank 1, column space a line l (7 lines, 7 matrices
each): modulo l, the 2 x 2 matrix Lbar and Lbar + Nbar must both be singular, where Nbar is N
modulo l, of rank 2 if l is not in R_P and 1 if it is. In both cases 6 matrices Lbar qualify (for
Nbar = I, the matrices with eigenvalues 0 and 1; for Nbar = E_11, the singular [[a, b], [c, d]]
with d = 0), and each has 4 lifts: 7 * 7 * 6 * 4 = 1176. Rank 0: all 64 L. So 1912 for each v, and
|Adm| = 176128 + 120 * 1912 - 2 = 405566 (removing 0 and A_u, which are counted).

*X_1 not inside H0.* X_1 = span(e_0, e_1, e_2, e_6). Let c_1, c_2 be A e_1, A e_2 (in R) and L the
3 x 3 matrix of A on D = span(e_3, e_4, e_5). A_u is 0 on e_1, e_2, e_6 and N = A_u on D is
invertible. Rank on X_1 at most 2 means dim span(c_1, c_2, v) <= 2. If v is in R: the singular
triples (c_1, c_2, v) in R, 344 of them, with L free: 344 * 512 = 176128. If v is not in R (120
choices): dim span(c_1, c_2) <= 1, and rank [c_1 c_2 L] <= 2 and rank [c_1 c_2 L + N] <= 2. With
c_1 = c_2 = 0, L and L + N must both be singular. For N = I, these are the matrices with
characteristic polynomial x^2 (x + 1) or x (x + 1)^2: 28 + 84 + 84 + 28 = 224. With span(c_1, c_2)
a line l (21 choices of (c_1, c_2)), the 2 x 3 matrices Lbar and Lbar + Nbar modulo l must have rank
at most 1, Nbar of rank 2. Normalising Nbar to [I | 0], the third column must be 0 (otherwise both
e_1 and e_2 lie on its line), which leaves the 2 x 2 count, 6, with 8 lifts each: 21 * 48 = 1008. So
1232 for each v, and |Adm| = 176128 + 120 * 1232 - 2 = 323966.

(c) g keeps X_1 and R (it commutes with A_u), fixes x0, and keeps P and H0. Choose a complement S'
of X_1; A_u restricts to an isomorphism iota : S' -> R, and from A_u g = g A_u, for s in S',
g s = iota^(-1)(a iota s) + b(s) with a = g on R and b(s) in X_1. For g s to lie in P when s does,
the S'-part must lie in P and b(s) in X_1 n P.

- X_1 inside H0: S' = span(e_4, e_5, e_6), S'_P = span(e_4, e_5), R_P = iota(S'_P). Then a must keep
  R_P, b(e_4) and b(e_5) lie in X_1 n P = span(e_1, e_2, e_3) (64 choices), and b(e_6) lies in X_1
  (16). Pair 0: R = span(e_0, e_1, e_2) and R n P = span(e_1, e_2). a fixes e_0 and keeps
  span(e_1, e_2), and g e_3 lies in e_3 + span(e_1, e_2) (4 choices). R_P is span(e_1, e_2) in c000
  (6 choices of a), span(e_1, e_0 + e_2) in c001 (a e_1 = e_1: 2) and span(e_0, e_2) in c002
  (a e_2 = e_2: 2). So |G| = 6 * 4 * 64 * 16 = 24576, then 8192 and 8192. Pair 1: R = X_1 n P and R_P =
  span(e_2, e_3), so a keeps a plane (24): 24 * 64 * 16 = 24576. Pair 2: R = span(e_3, e_2, e_0 + e_1),
  R n P = span(e_2, e_3). a keeps R n P, and then g e_1 = a(e_0 + e_1) + e_0 lies in P. In c000,
  R_P = R n P (24 choices), and in c001, R_P = span(e_2, e_0 + e_1 + e_3) (a e_2 = e_2, 2 * 2
  choices): 24576 and 4096.
- X_1 not inside H0: S' = span(e_3, e_4, e_5) lies in P, so a is only constrained on X_1, and b
  maps S' into X_1 n P = span(e_1, e_2) (64 choices). Pair 20: a fixes e_0 and keeps span(e_1, e_2)
  (6), and g e_6 lies in e_6 + R (8): 3072. Pair 31: a e_0 = e_0, a e_1 = e_1 (R n P = span(e_1)),
  a e_6 in e_6 + span(e_0, e_1) (4), and g e_2 in e_2 + span(e_1) (2): 512. Pair 32: a keeps
  R n P = span(e_1, e_2) (24): 1536. Pair 33: a e_2 = e_2 (R n P = span(e_2)), a(e_0 + e_1) in
  e_0 + e_1 + span(e_2) so that g e_1 lies in P (2), a e_6 in e_6 + span(e_2, e_0 + e_1) (4): 512.

(d) Each class of the action of G x Z_2 has at most 2 |G| elements. QED

As in Proposition 30, the class of (B5) is empty if and only if every instance with A_u and
A_(h_1) = the representative of a class of Adm fixed is UNSAT. Corollary 57 and the hint
conditions of job m42 hold in every instance; Adm uses only (a), so a finer set (for example with
Lemma 88 for w = h_1) could only make the counts smaller.

Guess (low confidence, not graded): 800 to 2000 classes for the nine open classes in all. For
Proposition 30 the bound ceil(|Adm| / (2 |G|)) was 2 in kind P against 40 classes, because maps
with large stabilisers dominate when G is large; for the classes with |G| = 512 the count should be
close to the bound.

Rule, written before m55 ran:
- A graded check fails: find the error in Proposition 32 before any count is used.
- All checks hold: no instance job for (B5) with d = 3 until job m53 or m54a shows that the split
  instances of (B6) close (at least 30 of m53's 40 or at least 60 of m54a's 89 UNSAT). Then the
  classes of (B5) with at most about 100 instances go first, one job per class, with the
  representative from m55's output; classes with several hundred instances (pair orbits 32 and 33)
  wait for a hand fact or a third fixed matrix. A class that m48b or m48c closes is dropped.
- If m53 and m54a show that the split does not close (B6) instances, this route is set aside for
  (B5) too.

## What does not follow

  did not close at 551 s, and the five classes of m46 with d = 3 did not close at 468 to 600 s.
- Proposition 32 is a count, not a constraint: it decides no class. Its (b) and (c) are by hand
  and are checked by machine only once job m55 has run.
