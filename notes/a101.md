## What changes

- **Every graded check held** (44 in m108g, 41 in m108h), and the 9 unit tests passed in each job.
- **The contradiction uses the "mixed" equations of (*), and not those inside N or inside the h
  part.** With the normal form and M kept, the deletion-minimal sets of the 16 groups of (*) have
  7 to 11 groups, not the 4 or fewer that would have made a hand proof easy. But they are nearly
  the same in all ten cases, across nine algebra types: six groups are kept in all ten (Q_vhh,
  Q_uhh, Q_uvh, L_vh, L_hu, L_uh), L_hv in nine and L_vu in eight, while Q_hhh, Q_vvh, Q_vvv and
  Q_uvv are dropped in all ten, and L_hh, L_vv, L_uv and L_uu in nine.
- **Three groups are needed in every case.** Dropping Q_vhh, Q_uhh or Q_uvh at its turn gave a
  model, verified outside the encoder, in all ten cases.
- **"M only" (m108h):** the twelve other k = 6 cases of the d = 3 classes that were UNSAT with no
  anisotropy in m107b/c are also UNSAT with (*), the normal form and M alone (38 to 86 s each). So
  on all sixteen such cases the two agree.
- **Job m109 (queued):** where the contradiction lives by basis index of V and by row of X, then by
  group, in the same ten cases.

**Observation (uncertified).** In these ten cases, the contradiction between (*), the normal form
and M does not use the equations of (*) that involve only N (indices u, v_1, v_2, v_3), nor Q_hhh:
it lives in the equations that tie N to the h part. The three groups (Q) with mixed kinds, Q_vhh,
Q_uhh and Q_uvh, are each needed in every case. This is about solver answers at a 120 s cap, and a
deletion-minimal set depends on the order tried; a different order could keep a different set.

## The decision rules

- **No verified model anisotropic on all of V:** nothing to re-check.
- **m108g/h, kept sets not small (7 to 11 groups) but nearly the same by type:** the precise target
  of Lemma 110 is not yet small enough to prove by hand. Grouping by kind of index is too coarse to
  say which h_j and which rows of X matter. Job m109 asks that: for each of the ten cases, a
  deletion-minimal set of basis indices of V, then of rows of X, then of groups (predictions in
  cube_groups/m109-predictions.md, written before the jobs ran).
- **m108h, "M only" UNSAT for all 12:** as the rule says, "M only" on the other cases with k > 4 is
  not needed; the hand proof is the target.
