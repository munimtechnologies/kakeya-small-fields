## Limits

- The UNSAT answers of m91a and m91b are solver answers saved without proof certificates. Only
  o005_c003's are certified so far.
- A VERIFIED answer certifies that one CNF has no solution. It does not certify that the CNF
  encodes the case it stands for: the encoding (m57.py), the hints, the unit clauses of Lemma 93 (b),
  Proposition 33's orbit roots and the reductions are code and hand arguments that no human has
  checked.
- An UNKNOWN is not evidence either way: the 45 count as open until m92 reports.
- No human mathematician has checked these results or any of the lemmas they use.
