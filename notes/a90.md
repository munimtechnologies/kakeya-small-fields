# A positive control at t = 8, and 20 of the 23 orbits of type (N4) out by a small reduced system

MV2 of Munim, Inc., 2026-10-24. Added to kakeya-small-fields next to [a89.md](a89.md). It was
researched and written with AI agents working in MV2's name. Job m97-square-identity-v1 ran on
2026-10-24 from 01:28 to 01:31 UTC, to the end. It tested Lemma 98 of [a89.md](a89.md) on solver
models, solved the small "reduced system" built from Lemma 98 for all 23 orbits of type (N4) at
(t, dim V) = (8, 11), and solved the CNF of each orbit without its anisotropy clauses ("relax"),
the positive control that job m95 lacked ([a88.md](a88.md)). This note grades it. **Lemma 100**
(by hand) rules out 14 of the 23 orbits. No result about A(8) is claimed yet: Proposition 37 waits
for job m98. The next free numbers are Lemma 101, Corollary 122, Proposition 37 and Theorem 34. No
human mathematician has checked the proofs. Text CC BY 4.0, code Apache-2.0.

## What changes

- **Every identity of Lemma 98 held on every model** the solver found: 6 models each at (4, 2) for
  M = N and E and at (6, 3) for (E_00, E_11) and (E_00, E_12), and 3 models each for 5 orbits at
  (8, 4). Each model was checked against (L) and (Q) (and (*) by brute force for t <= 6), the
  normal form, shape (c), every family of m95's conditions, and found not anisotropic.
- **The positive control now exists at t = 8.** Without anisotropy, the CNF of type (N4) has models
  for orbits 0, 1, 6, 14 and 29, each decided in under 0.4 s. So the encoder does not refute every
  rank-4 instance at t = 8 regardless of anisotropy. m94's pilot (orbit 5) is still UNSAT without
  anisotropy, as m95 and m96 found, and the reduced system does not explain that (it is SAT there).
- **With anisotropy, the reduced system is UNSAT for 20 of the 23 orbits.** 14 orbits contain a
  non-zero idempotent in W, which Lemma 99(c) forbids (Lemma 100 below, by hand). 6 orbits (20, 21,
  30, 35, 39, 41) have no invertible S for any of the 4096 choices of G, even without anisotropy.
  Orbits 18, 29 and 40 survive: their reduced systems are SAT at every level.
- **Two graded checks failed**, and both have the same cause: m97's search for an invertible S was
  incomplete. For orbits 0 and 14 the relax CNF has models, so the reduced system must be SAT, but
  it answered UNKNOWN. m97.invertible_in tried the elements c = 1, ..., 4096 of a solution space,
  which are the combinations of its first 12 basis vectors only, and these spaces have more. Its
  UNSAT answers are not affected: it reported UNSAT only when the whole space had been searched.
- **Job m98 (queued, predictions written first)** checks that the pair (G, S) read off each of 39
  solver models lies in the space the reduced system searches and solves it, decides the reduced
  system without a cap, gives d = 3 its positive control, and certifies m95b's 159 answers for d = 3
  with DRAT proofs.

## Lemma 100 (14 orbits of type (N4) with an idempotent)

**Lemma 100.** At t = 8 with an element u of rank 4 of type (N4), the space W = span{M_y : y in Y}
is not in any of m93's orbits 0, 1, 4, 5, 6, 7, 11, 14, 19, 25, 28, 34, 48 and 57.

*Proof.* In each of these orbits one vector of the recorded basis is a non-zero idempotent: E_00 in
orbits 0, 1, 4, 5, 6, 7, 11 and 14 (mask 1), E_00 + E_11 in orbit 25 (mask 33), E_00 + E_11 + E_22
in orbits 19, 28, 34 and 48 (mask 1057), and E_22 + E_33 in orbit 57 (mask 33792; mask bit 4 r + c
is entry (r, c)). By Lemma 96 the matrices M_(v_1), M_(v_2), M_(v_3) may be fixed to this basis, so
some M_y with y != 0 in Y is idempotent, which Lemma 99(c) rules out (n -> M_n is one-to-one by
Corollary 121). QED

This extends Lemma 99(c) from 8 orbits to 14, with the same proof. m97's reduced system, at stage
"q_zero", found exactly these 14 and no other orbit with z_y = y.

## The three survivors

    orbit 18:  W = span(E_01, E_02, E_03)            every product of two elements of W is 0
    orbit 29:  W = span(E_01, E_21, E_31)            every product of two elements of W is 0
    orbit 40:  W = span(E_01, E_21 + E_03, E_31 + E_02)    M_2 M_3 = M_3 M_2 = M_1, all squares 0

In all three every M_y squares to 0, so z_y = 0 for every y (Lemma 98(a)), T q(y) = y, and the
conditions of Lemma 98 come down to T M_n S y = (G_y + G_y^2) sigma(n). Orbits 18 and 29 are
transposes of each other up to conjugation (swap coordinates 0 and 1). Without anisotropy the CNF of orbit 29 has models, those of 18 and 40 do
not. With anisotropy all three are UNSAT in m95a with DRAT proofs VERIFIED by drat-trim, and the
control above shows that the encoder can return models at t = 8. A hand argument for these three
is the next target for type (N4).

## Where type (N4) stands

If job m98 confirms that every model's pair (G, S) solves its reduced system, type (N4) at (8, 11)
is closed, computer-assisted: 14 orbits by Lemma 100 (by hand), 6 by the reduced system (a complete
search over at most 4096 choices of G per orbit, with linear algebra over F_2), and 3 by m95a's
certified CNFs. That will be Proposition 37. The d = 3 half (159 instances of types (N3) and (F3),
UNSAT in m95b) still has no positive control and no certificates; m98 provides both. Then rank 4 at
(8, 11) would be ruled out, and A(8) <= 10 would still need the rank-3 configurations (B7)_8 and
(B6)_8 of Proposition 36.

## Limits
