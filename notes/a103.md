## The open cases

The open cases repeat the same few labels in class after class: t2 with M among m011, m012, m015,
m026, m030, m031, m034, m038, m043, m044, m047, m058, m059, m062 and m092, and t3 with (M_1, M_2)
among m004_034, m011_017, m012_032, m015_049, m021_047, m030_043 and m031_036. In m102's numbering a
3 x 3 matrix M is the integer with bit 3r + c set when M has a 1 at row r, column c, and a case's
basis matrices are the smallest of their space. So index < 64 means row 2 is zero, and since the
basis matrix is the smaller of M and M + I, all the open labels but m092 say: in every element of
T(N), the entries (2, 0) and (2, 1) are zero. That is, T(N) maps span(q_0, q_1) into itself, where
q_i = pi(e_(5 + i)).

In the four d = 1 pair orbits o007 to o010, Im A_u has the echelon bases (64, 32, 1), (64, 32, 2),
(64, 32, 3) and (64, 33, 2) (job m99), so pi(R) = span(q_0, q_1) in all 34 d = 1 classes. Hence:

**Observation (computer-assisted count, uncertified).** In shards a-g of m110, a d = 1 case with
k <= 4 stayed open only if T(N) leaves pi(R) invariant, with two exceptions (o008_c003 t2_m092 and
o010_c003 t2_m092). Conversely, of the cases of that form, eight were UNSAT at "n" (o007_c001
t2_m026, o007_c005 t3_m004_034, o007_c008 t2_m011 and t3_m011_017, o007_c009 t3_m011_017,
o009_c008 t2_m011 and t3_m011_017, o010_c004 t2_m011) and at least two more at the sweep.

At d = 2, pi(R) is the line span(q_0) in all four pair orbits o003 to o006, and T(N) leaves it
invariant when the entries (1, 0) and (2, 0) (bits 3 and 6) vanish. Read off m106's list, 27 of the
28 cases that m105 left UNKNOWN at 45 s are of that form; the exception is o005_c001 t3_m024_109.

Why it could be so: if T(N) leaves pi(R) invariant, then every A_n = A_u-bar T_n pi with n in N maps
the hyperplane R + X_1 of X into A_u(R), which has dimension 3 - d, so N acts on a large part of X
through a small space, and anisotropy on N alone is easier to satisfy (the SAT answers at "n" read so
far are all of this form). This is a heuristic, not a proof. The count above was read from instance names
and m102's bit order; job m111a recomputes it from A_u and the spaces directly, without the names.

## The decision rules

- **No verified model anisotropic on all of V:** nothing to re-check.
- **Some open (m110):** the rule asked to certify the UNSAT cases and re-run the open ones at the
  sweep with 600 s. Certification waits until shards h to j are back. Before a 600 s sweep on 315
  or more cases (about 53 CPU-hours), job m111 first tests, on one case per label, whether a
  smaller set of anisotropic vectors decides them, and checks the observation by machine.
- **Many cases SAT at "n" but UNSAT at the sweep:** did not fire (30 SAT at "n"; the sweep was
  UNKNOWN on all 30). What did happen: anisotropy outside N is needed at d = 1 for the cases that
  leave pi(R) invariant, as at d = 0.
