## Limits

- The new UNSAT answers are solver answers saved without proof certificates.
- Corollaries 92 to 95 rest on Proposition 33 (by hand, matched by machine in m66 and again in the
  checks of m76a-v2, m77a and m77b) and on Lemma 93 (a) (by hand, [a52.md](a52.md)) with the rank-0
  sweeps of job m64a. No human mathematician has checked them.
- An UNKNOWN is not evidence either way. o004_c001 is not counted as empty.
- A(7) <= 8 needs all 20 open classes of (B6) and all 3 of (B5) to be empty. Nothing here settles
  any pair (a, b) beyond those listed in earlier notes.
