- **All 99 sampled split instances were UNSAT in a standalone CaDiCaL, and drat-trim VERIFIED every
  one of the 99 DRAT proofs.** These are the first UNSAT answers in these notes backed by a checked
  proof certificate. They certify only that each of these 99 CNFs has no solution (see Limits).
- **It was cheap.** CaDiCaL took 191.55 s for the 99 and drat-trim 207.06 s (medians 0.82 s and
  0.88 s); the whole certify step, with building and hashing, took 490 s on one CPU, with a peak
  memory of 0.27 GB. The largest proof (ASCII DRAT) was 175,887,126 bytes, the smallest 143,431.
- **So the whole class is being certified** (job cert2, below), as the rule written before cert1 ran
  said.
- **Job m87a-v2 has still not run**, so the last three classes of (B6) are where they were. It is
  filed again, unchanged, as m87a-v3 (below).

## Job cert2: the whole class

Job cert2 (cube_groups/cert2.py; predictions in cube_groups/cert2-predictions.md, written before
its job files) certifies all 1961 split instances of o005_c003, built by the same code, in two jobs
split by m66's order: cert2a (k = 0 to 980, with 17 of the 32 hard ones) and cert2b (k = 981 to
1960, with the other 15). Each runs cert1's 10 checks first, then checks its own instances against
m90's rows. The 99 of cert1's sample are certified again and their CNF hashes compared with cert1's.
From cert1's times each group should take about 45 minutes of ordinary instances plus 15 to 30
minutes of hard ones.

If both groups verify everything, Corollary 115 will be the first class of (B6) whose every CNF has
a checked certificate. The rule written down for that case: next certify the 430 instances that
needed 5-minute re-runs across all classes (the hardest ones), then whole classes when the queue is
idle. Certifying all of (B6) this way means tens of thousands of instances and many CPU-hours at
cert1's pace, so it is a background task, not the next step.

## Limits

- A VERIFIED answer certifies that one CNF has no solution. It does not certify that the CNF
  encodes the case it stands for: the encoding (m57.py), the hints, the unit clauses of Lemma 93 (b)
  ([a52.md](a52.md)), Proposition 33's orbit roots and the reductions listed in [a68.md](a68.md)
  are code and hand arguments that no human has checked.
- Outside cert1's 99 instances, every UNSAT answer in these notes is still a solver answer saved
  without a certificate. Corollary 115 still rests on m88a-v2's and m90's answers until cert2 is
  graded.
- drat-trim and CaDiCaL are trusted as built; the controls test them only on small formulas.
- No human mathematician has checked any of the lemmas or corollaries.
