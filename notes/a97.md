## Proposition 38

Notation as in [a93.md](a93.md) to [a96.md](a96.md): u is in (B7)_8 at t = 8, dim V = 11, in the
rank-0 case of Lemma 103, N = ker phi, Q = X / X_1, and T(N) is the commuting space of Lemma 104.
k(T(N)) is the number of subspaces of Q that every element of T(N) maps into themselves.

**Proposition 38 (computer-assisted).** If the pair orbit of u is o000, o001 or o002
(d = dim(X_1 n R) = 3), then k(T(N)) >= 6. Equivalently, T(N) is span(I), span(I, M) with M of
type nil1, proj or x^2(x+1), or a 3-dimensional algebra of type F_2^3, F_2 x F_2[x]/(x^2),
rad2_image or rad2_kernel (Lemma 108); it is never of type irr, nil3, x(x^2+x+1), F_8,
F_2 x F_4 or F_2[x]/(x^3).

*Proof.* By Lemma 106 and the Kbar reduction of job m102, a system of this kind gives, after a
change of basis that keeps the normal form, a model of the CNF of one case in m102.class_cases
of its class (o000_c000, o001_c000 or o002_c000, the three classes with d = 3). The cases of these
classes with k <= 4 are exactly the 57 of job m104 (37, 6 and 14; checked in every job, and
again by the count, which finds 57 at d = 3), and their types are those of Lemma 108 (checked by
machine in job m103). Each of the 57 CNFs is unsatisfiable, with a DRAT proof verified by
drat-trim. QED

What it rests on: the hand lemmas that set up the CNF (Lemmas 102 to 106 and 108, none checked by
a human), the encoders m42, m100, m101 and m102, and Theorems 27 and 33 (computer-assisted). A
DRAT certificate checks the CNF, not the encoding.

**Observation (uncertified).** In each of the six cases, anisotropy on N and on the seven cosets
h + N with h in span(h_5, h_6, h_7) already contradicts the hand conditions. So does anisotropy on
phi^(-1)(X_1) = N + span(h_1, ..., h_4), which shares only N with the first W. So a refutation does
not need anisotropy on most of V: either 127 or 255 of its 2,047 non-zero vectors will do. By
m104's rule this is the first target for a hand proof, starting from Lemma 109. No hand proof was
found in this run. Job m105e first asks how small W can be: N alone, N plus one h, the h without N.

**The failed level.** The third level, "out3_in1" (out3 plus h_1), stopped with KeyError (8, 4) in
all six cases before any solving. m104.level_idx put index 4 (h_1) after 8, 9, 10, and
m101.add_aniso_on reads the variables of beta(h_i, h_j) by pairs (i, j) with i < j taken in list
order, so the out-of-order list asked for a pair that does not exist. The other levels' lists are
increasing, and the cert part does not use them. That level's CNF would contain every clause of the
out3 CNF, so it is UNSAT whenever out3 is: no answer was lost. test_m104 asserted the unsorted list
because its expected value was written from level_idx, not from what add_aniso_on needs. m105
sorts every index list and tests that.
