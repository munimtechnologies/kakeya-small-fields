## Corollary 55 (no element of rank 4 for t = 7)

**Corollary 55 (computer-assisted).** No anisotropic system with t = 7 and dim V = 9 has an element
of rank 4.

*Proof.* Let u have rank 4. By Corollary 52 ([a26.md](a26.md)) u is not in Case A; by Lemma 42
([case-b-shapes.md](case-b-shapes.md)) it is not in shape (d1); by Corollary 54 ([a34.md](a34.md))
it is not in shape (d2). So u is in Case B, shape (c), and by Lemmas 35 and 37 the system has the
normal form of [a29.md](a29.md), with ker A_u = W and, by Corollary 51 ([a24.md](a24.md)),
dim(R n W) >= 2 for R = Im A_u and n -> A_n one-to-one. Every g in K = {g : g e_0 = e_0, g W = W,
g P = P} keeps this form, so R may be replaced by one of the 5 representatives of Lemma 85 (the
orbit closure under K confirmed them, "selfcheck_hand_r_orbits"), and then A_u by any conjugate
under Stab_K(R). The 20160 matrices with kernel W and image R fall into classes under random units
of Stab_K(R); every unit was checked to lie in Stab_K(R), so each computed class lies inside one
true class and the 54 classes cover every A_u. Each class gives one CNF (the encoding of
case_b_shapes for shape (c) plus the unit clauses for A_u, the commuting matrices M_n of Lemma 38
and the conditions of Corollary 51), and a model of the system would be a model of the CNF of its
class. Jobs m44a to m44d found all 54 UNSAT (CaDiCaL 1.9.5, no proof certificates saved). QED

What it rests on: Lemmas 35, 37 and 38 (by hand), Corollary 51 (by hand; it uses A(6) <= 7), Lemma
85 (by hand, matched by machine), the encoding of case_b.py and case_b_shapes.py (whose selfchecks
and controls held here and in jobs m23, m25, m40, m43), 54 solver answers here, and through
Corollaries 52 and 54 another 6 + 210 solver answers (jobs m39 and m43). It does not rest on Lemma
86: the class count is the machine's, and a missed generator would only have made more classes,
each still solved.
