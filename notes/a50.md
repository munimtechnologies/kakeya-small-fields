## Corollary 62 (the kind-graph class of (B6) is empty)

**Corollary 62.** Let a system with t = 7 and dim V = 9 be anisotropic. Then no element of rank 3
lies in the class of (B6) with d = 3 whose Im A_u neither lies in P nor contains x0 (pair orbit 2,
A_u = e_4 -> e_3, e_5 -> e_2, e_6 -> e_0 + e_1 in m42's coordinates).

*Proof (computer-assisted).* By Corollary 55 there is no element of rank 4. Suppose u is such an
element. By Proposition 30 (a) ([a42.md](a42.md)), a second element h_1 of ker phi has A_(h_1) in
Adm, and up to the stabiliser of (x0, P, A_u) and h_1 -> h_1 + u, A_(h_1) is one of 178
representatives (counted by hand in Proposition 30, matched by machine in job m52). With A_u and
A_(h_1) fixed and m42's hints added, each of the 178 instances is UNSAT: 88 in job m54a, a025 and
87 of batch b in m58a, and a117 and a153 here. So no such u exists.

This rests on 178 solver answers saved without proof certificates, on Proposition 30 and on the
hand lemmas behind m42's hints (Lemma 88 and the earlier ones listed in [a36.md](a36.md)). With
Corollary 61 ([a47.md](a47.md)), two of the three classes of (B6) with d = 3 are empty.
