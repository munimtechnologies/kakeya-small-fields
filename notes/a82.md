## What changes

- **All 49 instances of job m89 have DRAT proofs VERIFIED by drat-trim.** These are the 49 split
  instances of the (B6) classes o003_c001 and o005_c001 that job m87b left open, and whose UNSAT
  answers in job m89 closed both classes (Corollaries 113 and 114, [a74.md](a74.md)). A standalone
  CaDiCaL (rel-2.2.1) found each UNSAT and wrote a DRAT proof, and drat-trim checked it: cert4a 25
  of 25 VERIFIED, cert4b 24 of 24. By class: o003_c001 32 of 32 (16 + 16), o005_c001 17 of 17
  (9 + 8). Nothing was SAT, UNKNOWN or SKIPPED. Every graded check held (18 of 18 in cert4a, 17 of
  17 in cert4b), among them cert1's controls and m89's own variable, clause, hint and unit counts
  for the first instance of each kind, so the certified CNFs are the ones m89 solved.
- **What is now certified behind Theorem 33:** the 1961 split instances of o005_c003 (cert2, all of
  that class), m92's 45 (cert3) and these 49, so 2055 distinct instances. 126 of the 495 5-minute
  re-runs are among them (m90's 32 inside o005_c003, m92's 45, m89's 49). Every certificate run so
  far has come back VERIFIED: 2154 of 2154 (cert1's 99 sample instances were certified twice).
  Every other solver answer behind Theorem 33, including the other 1567 + 1896 split instances of
  o003_c001 and o005_c001, is still saved without a certificate.
- **Job cert5 is queued:** the 45 re-runs of job m87, which closed o010_c001 (Corollary 116,
  [a78.md](a78.md)), o010_c008 and o005_c002 (Corollaries 111 and 112, [a73.md](a73.md)).

The per-instance times, proof sizes and the SHA-256 of every certified CNF are in
cube_groups/results/cert4a and cert4b (grade.json, "rows"; certify.log). The proofs themselves
(up to 268 MB each) were deleted after checking; anyone can rebuild a CNF with cube_groups/cert4.py,
compare its hash, and re-run CaDiCaL and drat-trim.

Graded against cube_groups/cert4-predictions.md, written before the job files. Every graded check
held. The exploratory guesses:

- Every instance of both groups UNSAT and VERIFIED, none SKIPPED (about 90%): held.
- Each group's certify step at most 45 minutes (about 80%): held (31 and 38 minutes).
- Every CaDiCaL run within its 400 s cap (about 97%): held (slowest 125.10 s).
- drat-trim's total at most 1.5 times CaDiCaL's in each group (about 85%): held (0.94 and 0.97).
- Standalone CaDiCaL's total at most m89's python-sat total in each group (about 65%): **missed in
  both groups**: 957.87 s against 831.12 s (1.15 times) in cert4a, 1139.23 s against 964.65 s
  (1.18 times) in cert4b.
- Largest proof at most 500 MB (about 85%): held (268 MB).

Five held and one missed. The miss changes no claim: it is about speed, not answers. In cert3 the
same ratio was 0.93 and 0.60, so over four groups it has ranged from 0.60 to 1.18. The two runs
differ in CaDiCaL version, options (proof logging on) and machine load, so neither a speed-up nor a
slow-down is claimed; cert5's guess on this is a band, 0.8 to 1.4.

Under cert4-predictions.md's rule ("both groups all VERIFIED"), the next step is the other 5-minute

row format, so cube_groups/cert5.py is cert4.py with m87's rows. m87.py keeps its class lists per

recomputes the roots with m87's seed in m87's order. Its predictions are in
cube_groups/cert5-predictions.md, written before the job files.

## Limits

- A VERIFIED certificate covers one CNF only. The encoding (m57.py on sat_aniso.py), the hints,
  Lemma 93's unit clauses, the orbit representatives of Propositions 30 to 34 and the reductions
  are code and hand arguments that no human has checked.
- Theorem 33 stays computer-assisted. It rests on many thousands of solver answers, of which 2055
  now carry checked certificates.
- No human mathematician has checked Theorem 33 or any lemma it uses. Until someone does, read it
  as a claim with its evidence, not as an established result.
