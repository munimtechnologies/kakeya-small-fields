## What changes

- **Proposition 30 (a).** In a class of (B6) with d = 3, every n in N = ker phi outside span(u)
  has a map A_n in the set **Adm** of maps A with A x0 = 0, A(X) inside R, rank of A on X_1 at most
  2, A != 0 and A != A_u. (The last two are Corollary 60; the rank bound holds because A_n kills
  the two independent vectors x0 and sigma(n) of X_1.)
- **Proposition 30 (b), (c).** Up to the stabiliser G of (x0, P, A_u) and n -> n + u, Adm has
  **40 classes when R lies in P (kind P)** and **178 when R is neither inside P nor through x0
  (kind graph)**. By rank of A on X_1 (0, 1, 2): 6, 20, 14 and 18, 94, 66. |G| is 86016 and
  12288. Both counts are by hand, with Burnside's lemma; the kind with x0 in R (kind L) was not
  counted by hand.
- So the kind-P class of (B6) (pair orbit 1, tried only for about 250 s in job m42b) splits into
  40 instances with A_u and A_(h_1) both fixed, in which (*) and (Q) become linear in many more
  unknowns. At t = 6 the analogous split (Lemma 71, [a18.md](a18.md)) was prepared but not needed.
- **Job m52** (cube_groups/m52.py, predictions in cube_groups/m52-predictions.md, written before
  it ran) finds G by solving its linear conditions, not from the parametrisation below, and counts
  the classes of Adm for all three kinds by union-find. It takes minutes, so it was queued
  2026-10-20 although five larger jobs are pending.

## Setting

As in [a36.md](a36.md) and [a39.md](a39.md): t = 7, dim V = 9, the system is anisotropic, there
is no element of rank 4 (Corollary 55), and u is an element of rank 3 in (B6): x0 = sigma(u) is
not in P = phi(V), P is a hyperplane of X, and N = ker phi = U+ has dimension 3. X_1 = ker A_u
(dimension 4, containing x0), R = Im A_u (dimension 3), and d = dim(X_1 n R) = 3, that is, R lies
in X_1 and A_u^2 = 0. By Lemma 82's argument ([a36.md](a36.md)) there is one class of A_u for
each kind: R inside P (kind P, pair orbit 1), x0 in R (kind L, pair orbit 0), or neither (kind
graph, pair orbit 2). G is the group of g in GL(X) with g x0 = x0, g P = P and g A_u = A_u g:
the base changes of X that keep the normal form of u. Any g in G can be followed by a new choice
of basis of V, so a system in the class and its image under g are both in the class.

## Proposition 30

**Proposition 30.** Let u be in (B6) with d = 3.

(a) For every n in N outside span(u), A_n is in Adm.

(b) Let R be inside P or neither inside P nor through x0. Then X_1 = R + span(x0). Choose a
    complement S of X_1 n P in P, so that A_u maps S onto R, and identify S with R through A_u.
    A map A in Adm is a pair (alpha, beta) of endomorphisms of R, alpha = A on R (singular) and
    beta = A on S; A_u is (0, I). In kind P, G consists of the maps g = (a, b) with g = a on R,
    g x0 = x0 and g(s) = b(s) + a(s) on S, for a in GL(R) and b : S -> R, so
    |G| = 168 * 512 = 86016. In kind graph, P n X_1 = {r + lambda(r) x0 : r in R} for a non-zero
    linear form lambda on R, a must keep lambda (24 choices), b maps S into P n X_1 (512 choices),
    and |G| = 24 * 512 = 12288. In both, g sends (alpha, beta) to

        (a alpha a^(-1),  a beta a^(-1) + (a alpha a^(-1)) c)       with c = (R-part of b) a^(-1),

    and c runs over all of End(R). So the class of A is that of alpha under conjugation by the
    centraliser of alpha, together with betabar = pi beta in Hom(R, R / Im alpha) (pi the
    projection), on which a in C(alpha) acts by betabar -> abar betabar a^(-1). The move
    n -> n + u adds pi to betabar.

(c) The number of classes of Adm under G and n -> n + u is 40 in kind P and 178 in kind graph:

        alpha (similarity class)    rank   |C(alpha)|   size of W   kind P   kind graph
        0                             0        168        512          6         18
        J_2(0) + 0                    1          8         64         10         44
        diag(1, 0, 0)                 1          6         64         10         50
        J_3(0)                        2          4          8          3         11
        J_2(0) + 1                    2          2          8          4         20
        0 + C(x^2 + x + 1)            2          3          8          2         10
        0 + J_2(1)                    2          2          8          3         17
        diag(0, 1, 1)                 2          6          8          2          8
                                                                      40        178

    W is Hom(R, R / Im alpha), the space of betabar (for alpha = 0, beta itself). By rank:
    6, 20, 14 in kind P and 18, 94, 66 in kind graph.

*Proof.* (a) By Proposition 29 (c), A_n x0 = 0 and A_n maps P + span(x0) = X into R. By (N) of
a36.md, sigma(n) lies in X_1, and by Lemma 30 (b) for u it is not in span(x0); A_n sigma(n) = 0
(Lemma 21 (b)). So the kernel of A_n on X_1 contains the plane span(x0, sigma(n)), and A_n has
rank at most 2 on X_1. A_n != 0 and A_(n + u) = A_n + A_u != 0 by Corollary 60 (d = 3 >= 1).

(b) As x0 is not in R, X_1 = R + span(x0). An element g of G keeps X_1 = ker A_u and R = Im A_u,
fixes x0 and keeps P. On X_1 it is a on R and fixes x0. On S, g(s) lies in P = (X_1 n P) + S; its
S-part is fixed by A_u g = g A_u (it is a, under the identification), and its (X_1 n P)-part b(s)
is free. In kind P, X_1 n P = R and every a in GL(R) occurs. In kind graph, write P = ker f with
f(x0) = 1 and lambda = f on R (non-zero, as R is not in P); then X_1 n P is the graph of lambda,
and g keeps P exactly when lambda(a r) = lambda(r) for all r, a stabiliser of order 168 / 7 = 24.
A map A in Adm kills x0, so it is determined by alpha and beta, and alpha is singular (rank on
X_1 at most 2). For the action, compute g A g^(-1) on R and on S: on R it is a alpha a^(-1); on S,
g^(-1) s = a^(-1) s plus a vector of X_1 n P whose R-part is a^(-1) b a^(-1) s, which A sends to
alpha of that R-part (A kills x0). This gives the formula. Since b runs over all maps S -> X_1 n P,
which projects isomorphically onto R, c runs over End(R); adding (a alpha a^(-1)) c to beta changes
exactly the columns modulo Im(a alpha a^(-1)), so the invariant of beta is betabar. For a in
C(alpha), Im alpha is kept, abar is defined on R / Im alpha, and pi a = abar pi, so the move by
A_u (betabar -> betabar + pi) commutes with the action.

(c) Burnside's lemma for the group C(alpha) x Z_2 (the Z_2 is n -> n + u) on W in kind P, and on
pairs (lambda, betabar) with lambda a non-zero form on R in kind graph (a acts on lambda by
lambda -> lambda a^(-1)); in the graph count the group is C(alpha) in GL(R), and the classes of
(alpha, lambda) under GL(R) replace the classes of alpha under the stabiliser of lambda. The number
of forms fixed by a is 2^(dim ker(a - I)) - 1.

- alpha = 0: W = End(R) under conjugation. The 14 similarity classes of 3 x 3 matrices over F_2
  fall into 7 pairs {beta, beta + I}: (0, I), (J_2(0) + 0, J_2(1) + 1), (J_3(0), J_3(1)),
  (diag(0, 0, 1), diag(0, 1, 1)), (J_2(0) + 1, 0 + J_2(1)), (0 + C, 1 + C) with C = C(x^2 + x + 1),
  and the two cubics x^3 + x + 1 and x^3 + x^2 + 1. Without (0, I) (Corollary 60): 6. In kind
  graph, each pair adds the orbits of C(beta) on non-zero forms: 3, 3, 3, 5, 3 and 1, so 18.
- alpha = J_2(0) + 0 (alpha e_2 = e_1, alpha e_1 = alpha e_3 = 0): C(alpha) is the 8 matrices
  I + x E_12 + y E_13 + z E_32, abar = [[1, 0], [z, 1]] on R / span(e_1). Fixed points on W (64):
  without the move, 64 for a = I, 16 for each of the 3 with z = 0 and (x, y) != 0, 8 for the 2
  with z = 1, y = 0, and 4 for the 2 with z = 1, y = 1; with the move, 0, 0, 8 and 4. Kind P:
  (64 + 48 + 16 + 8 + 16 + 8) / 16 = 10. The non-zero fixed forms are 7, 3, 3 and 1, so kind
  graph: (448 + 144 + 48 + 8 + 48 + 8) / 16 = 44.
- alpha = diag(1, 0, 0): C(alpha) = GL_2 on span(e_2, e_3); betabar = (v, M), v in F_2^2 and M a
  2 x 2 matrix, acted on by (gv, g M g^(-1)), and the move adds I to M. Fixed points (identity,
  3 involutions, 2 elements of order 3): 64, 8, 4 without the move and 0, 8, 0 with it (no M has
  g M g^(-1) = M + I for g of order 3, as I is not orthogonal to the centraliser F_4 under the
  trace form). Kind P: 120 / 12 = 10. Fixed non-zero forms 7, 3, 1; kind graph: 600 / 12 = 50.
- Rank 2: W = R* (8 forms), abar = 1, the move adds the form pi with kernel Im alpha. Kind P,
  orbits of C(alpha) on R* paired by the move: J_3(0): {0, pi}, {f(e_1) = 0, f(e_2) = 1},
  {f(e_1) = 1}: 3; J_2(0) + 1: 4; 0 + C: 2; 0 + J_2(1): 3; diag(0, 1, 1): 2. Kind graph by
  Burnside: 88 / 8 = 11, 80 / 4 = 20, 60 / 6 = 10, 68 / 4 = 17, 96 / 12 = 8.

The class sizes check: 1 + 21 + 28 = 50 matrices of rank at most 1 and 42 + 84 + 56 + 84 + 28 =
294 of rank 2, 344 singular matrices in all, so |Adm| = 344 * 512 - 2 = 176126 in every kind. QED

**What (c) is for.** In job m42's normal form the basis of V starts with u = h_0 and h_1, h_2
spanning N with u. Given a system in the class, Proposition 30 (a) puts A_n in Adm for every n in
N outside span(u); a base change g in G (followed by a new basis of V) and, if needed, the swap of
h_1 and h_1 + u bring A_(h_1) to the chosen representative of its class. So the kind-P class is
empty if and only if each of 40 instances, with A_u and A_(h_1) both fixed, is unsatisfiable;
the same holds with 178 for kind graph. This is valid only if m42.py's normal form uses no
symmetry beyond x0, P and A_u (for example, no normalisation of sigma(h_1) or of beta on N); that
is to be read in m42.py before any instance job is built. Fixing A_(h_1) makes every condition
A_(h_1) phi(h) = A_u beta(h_1, h) of (N) linear in the unknown values of beta, and (*) with
h' = h_1 linear in the unknown A_h.

**Kind L.** When x0 is in R, X_1 / span(x0) is not R: A on X_1 is a map from T = X_1 n P to R,
two different 3-dimensional spaces that share the plane R n P, and G acts on it from both sides
(through GL(R n P) and the 4 maps of T fixing R n P and moving the third basis vector within its
coset). The count was not done by hand; job m52 counts it.

## What does not follow

- Nothing is decided for (B6) or (B5): 14 of 68 and 350 of 420 classes are UNSAT. 7 <= A(7) <= 9.
- Proposition 30 is a count, not a constraint: it decides no class. Whether fixing A_(h_1) makes
  the d = 3 classes solvable has not been measured.
- Proposition 30 (a) uses Corollary 60 (by hand on Corollary 55, which is computer-assisted) and
  Lemma 30; (b) and (c) are linear algebra and counting by hand, checked by machine only once job
  m52 has run.
- The same split for the ten classes of (B5) with d = 3 is larger (there A_n is free on a vector
  outside P + span(x0)) and was not counted.
