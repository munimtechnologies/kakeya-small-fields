## What changes

- **All 45 instances of job m92 have DRAT proofs VERIFIED by drat-trim.** These are the 45 split
  instances of the (B6) classes o003_c000 and o005_c000 that jobs m91a and m91b left open, and
  whose UNSAT answers in job m92 closed (B6) (Corollaries 117 and 118). A standalone CaDiCaL
  (rel-2.2.1) found each UNSAT and wrote a DRAT proof, and drat-trim checked it: cert3a 25 of 25
  VERIFIED, cert3b 20 of 20. Nothing was SAT, UNKNOWN or SKIPPED. Every graded check held (13 of 13
  in cert3a, 14 of 14 in cert3b), among them cert1's controls and m92's own variable, clause, hint
  and unit counts for the first instance of each kind, so the certified CNFs are the ones m92 solved.
- **What is now certified behind Theorem 33:** the 1961 split instances of o005_c003 (cert2, all of
  that class) and these 45, so 2006 distinct instances. 77 of the 495 5-minute re-runs are among
  them (m90's 32 inside o005_c003, and m92's 45). Every other solver answer behind Theorem 33,
  including the other 1574 + 1893 split instances of o003_c000 and o005_c000, is still saved
  without a certificate.
- **Job cert4 is queued:** the 49 re-runs of job m89, which closed o003_c001 and o005_c001
  (Corollaries 113 and 114, [a74.md](a74.md)).

The per-instance times, proof sizes and the SHA-256 of every certified CNF are in
cube_groups/results/cert3a and cert3b (grade.json, "rows"; certify.log). The proofs themselves
(up to 276 MB each) were deleted after checking; anyone can rebuild a CNF with cube_groups/cert3.py,
compare its hash, and re-run CaDiCaL and drat-trim.

Graded against cube_groups/cert3-predictions.md, written before the job files. Every graded check
held. The exploratory guesses:

- Every instance of both groups UNSAT and VERIFIED, none SKIPPED (about 85%): held.
- Each group's certify step at most 90 minutes (about 75%): held (41 and 21 minutes).
- Every CaDiCaL run within its 400 s cap (about 90%): held (slowest 112.46 s).
- drat-trim's total at most 1.5 times CaDiCaL's in each group (about 70%): held (1.08 and 1.11).
- Standalone CaDiCaL's median within a factor of 2 of m92's (about 60%): held in cert3a (45.25 s
  against 45.56 s). In cert3b the ratio is 49.78 / 24.89 = 2.00 at the printed precision of
  0.01 s, exactly on the boundary; it is counted as held only in the sense that "within" includes
  equality, and should not be read as a clear hit.
- Largest proof at most 1 GB (about 80%): held (276 MB).

Five clear hits and one on the boundary. An observation, not a claim: standalone CaDiCaL with proof
logging took 0.93 times m92's python-sat time in cert3a and 0.60 times in cert3b (the two rank-1
instances 2.68 s and 5.00 s against 17.59 s and 14.51 s). The two runs differ in CaDiCaL version,
options and machine load, so no speed-up is claimed.

## Limits

- A VERIFIED certificate covers one CNF only. The encoding (m57.py on sat_aniso.py), the hints,
  Lemma 93's unit clauses, the orbit representatives of Propositions 30 to 34 and the reductions
  are code and hand arguments that no human has checked.
- Theorem 33 stays computer-assisted. It rests on many thousands of solver answers, of which 2006
  now carry checked certificates.
- No human mathematician has checked Theorem 33 or any lemma it uses. Until someone does, read it
  as a claim with its evidence, not as an established result.
