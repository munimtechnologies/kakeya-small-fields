## Corollary 61 (computer-assisted)

**Corollary 61.** Let a system with t = 7 and dim V = 9 have an element u of rank 3 in
configuration (B6), with ker A_u = X_1 and Im A_u = R in pair orbit 1 of Lemma 83 (kind P:
R = span(e_1, e_2, e_3) lies in P, d = dim(X_1 n R) = 3). Then the system is not anisotropic. In
other words, the class r3b_t7_B6_o001_c000 of job m42 is empty.

*Proof.* Lemma 83 and job m42 put A_u in one class up to base change, with representative
e_4 -> e_1, e_5 -> e_2, e_6 -> e_3. By Proposition 30 (a) ([a42.md](a42.md), by hand), a second
element h_1 of ker phi has its map A_(h_1) in the set Adm, and up to the stabiliser of
(x0, P, A_u) and h_1 -> h_1 + u that map is one of 40 representatives. Job m52 confirmed the count
40 (6 / 20 / 14 by rank on X_1) by machine ([a46.md](a46.md)). For each representative, the
instance is job m42's CNF for that A_u with its 79 hint conditions and 49 unit clauses fixing
A_(h_1). Job m53 found 39 of the 40 UNSAT, and job m57b found the last one, a07, UNSAT. So no
choice of A_(h_1) completes to an anisotropic system. ∎

It rests on 40 solver answers saved without proof certificates (jobs m53 and m57b), on
Proposition 30 (a) and Lemma 83 (by hand) with m52's machine count, and on the hints of m42.py
((2) and (3) of a10.md). The planted positive control at t = 6 with A_(v_1) fixed was found SAT
and verified in m53, and in both arms in m57b.
