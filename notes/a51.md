## Corollary 63 (two classes of (B5) with d = 3 are empty)

**Corollary 63.** Let a system with t = 7 and dim V = 9 be anisotropic. Then no element u of rank 3
in (B5) has A_u in either of these two classes (m42's coordinates, x0 = e_0, P = span(e_1, ...,
e_5); ker A_u = span(e_0, ..., e_3) in both):

    o001_c000   A_u = e_4 -> e_3, e_5 -> e_2, e_6 -> e_1          Im A_u = span(e_1, e_2, e_3), inside P
    o002_c000   A_u = e_4 -> e_3, e_5 -> e_2, e_6 -> e_0 + e_1    Im A_u = span(e_0 + e_1, e_2, e_3)

*Proof (computer-assisted).* By Corollary 55 there is no element of rank 4. Suppose u is such an
element. By Proposition 32 (a) ([a45.md](a45.md)), a second element h_1 of ker phi outside span(u)
has A_(h_1) in Adm, and up to the stabiliser G of (x0, P, A_u) and h_1 -> h_1 + u, A_(h_1) is one of
445 representatives for o001_c000 and 421 for o002_c000 (job m55 counted them by union-find, with
|G| = 24576 and |Adm| = 405566 matching the hand counts of Proposition 32; m60's check counted them
again). With A_u and A_(h_1) fixed and m42's hints added, every one of these instances is UNSAT:
for o001_c000, 436 in job m59b and 9 in m60a; for o002_c000, 404 in m59b, 4 in m60a and 13 in
m60b. So no such u exists.

This rests on 866 solver answers saved without proof certificates, on Proposition 32 and on the
hand lemmas behind m42's hints (Lemma 88 and the earlier ones listed in [a36.md](a36.md)). It is
the (B5) counterpart of Corollary 62: o002_c000 has the same A_u as the kind-graph class of (B6)
([a50.md](a50.md)), there with P = span(e_1, ..., e_6).
