## Setting (from Lemma 84)

X = F_2^7 with basis e_0, ..., e_6; W = ker A_u = span(e_0, e_1, e_2); Y = span(e_1, e_2) = W n P;
P = span(e_1, ..., e_6); delta = e_2 (form d2c0) or e_0 + e_2 (form d2c1); R = Im A_u of dimension
4 with delta in R;

    K = {g in GL(X) : g e_0 = e_0, g e_1 = e_1, g e_2 = e_2, g P = P},   |K| = 2^8 x 20160 = 5160960,

in blocks g = [I_3 M; 0 A] with A in GL_4 and M a 3 x 4 matrix whose e_0-row is zero, so that M is
any linear map from Q = X / W = span([e_3], ..., [e_6]) into Y. By Lemma 84, R is one of 7 (d2c0) or
5 (d2c1) representatives, and A_u is fixed up to conjugation by Stab_K(R).

The notation is that of a30.md: R_W = R n W, r = dim R_W, Rbar = (R + W) / W in Q, of dimension
4 - r; A_u is the same as an isomorphism L : Q -> R; conjugation by g in Stab_K(R) replaces L by

    L' = g|_R . L . A^(-1),                                                       (C)

and T = pi . L : Q -> Q (pi : R -> R / R_W = Rbar) changes by T' = A T A^(-1), with image Rbar and
kernel Z = L^(-1)(R_W). Choose a complement R_1 of R_W in R and write rho : R -> R_W for the
projection along R_1, and lambda = rho . L : Q -> R_W. L is fixed by (T, lambda), and L is
invertible exactly when lambda|_Z : Z -> R_W is.

**The difference from shape (c).** Here g fixes W pointwise, so g|_(R_W) is always the identity:
the map "c" of a30.md, Step 3, is trivial. What g does to lambda is

    lambda' = (lambda + m . T) . A^(-1),                                          (L)

where g|_R = [I m; 0 A|_Rbar] on R = R_W (+) R_1 and m : R_1 -> R_W comes from M. Which m occur
depends on the orbit of R. As in a30.md, everything below counts orbits of pairs (T, extra data).

R is given by R_W, Rbar and a map f : Rbar -> W / R_W (Lemma 84, Step 2), and g = [I M; 0 A] keeps R
exactly when A Rbar = Rbar and

    f(A x) - f(x) = M x  mod R_W   for x in Rbar.                                 (S)

## How job m43 checks this

m43.py writes out/selfcheck.json and the classes to out/classes_t7_d2c0.json and
classes_t7_d2c1.json. Lemma 87 is graded by:

1. At t = 4, 5, 6 the classes are exact (the whole stabiliser is listed and compared):
   `exact["t6_d2c0"]["classes"]` must be 33, `exact["t6_d2c1"]["classes"]` 26, `exact["t5_d2c0"]`
   7, `exact["t5_d2c1"]` 6, `exact["t4_d2c0"]` and `exact["t4_d2c1"]` 1. A different number means
   Lemma 87 is wrong there (the note is then corrected; m43's solver answers are unaffected).
2. At t = 7, `split[form]["per_r"]`, matched to the orbits by `split[form]["invariants"]`
   (dim_R_W, dim_R_Y, e0_in_R, R_in_P) or by the orbit sizes in classes_t7_<form>.json: d2c0 dim_R_W
   3 -> 9; dim_R_W 2 with dim_R_Y 2 -> 10 if R_in_P, 22 if not; dim_R_W 2 with dim_R_Y 1 -> 18 each;
   dim_R_W 1 -> 11 if R_in_P, 31 if not. d2c1: dim_R_W 3 -> 9, dim_R_W 2 -> 18 each, dim_R_W 1 ->
   28. The machine classes are closures under 24 random units, so the machine count is **at least**
   the true one, and equal when the units generate the stabiliser. A machine count below the hand
   count means Lemma 87 is wrong; a count above it with `stable` true is a warning to look at the
   sizes: a true class has one of the sizes in the table, a split one a proper divisor.
3. The class sizes in classes_t7_<form>.json, by orbit, must be the multisets of the table (same rule
   on splitting).
4. (Recorded) m43a and m43b share 119 classes, m43c and m43d 91, so 1.6 to 3 minutes per class:
   UNKNOWN classes would be no surprise and would go to a follow-up job with 3 times the budget.

## What does not follow

- Nothing about A(7) <= 8: Lemma 87 counts instances; it decides none.
- Lemma 87 is by hand and unchecked by machine until m43 reports. Its sizes and totals pass the checks
  of Step 5, and two of its rows reuse the orbit counts of types X and Yn of Lemma 86, which job m44
  has not checked yet either; a wrong orbit count there would show in both jobs, not in these sums.
