**Observation (uncertified).** In each of the six core cases, the conditions of the case together
with sigma(n) != 0 for the 15 non-zero n in N are contradictory. So are the conditions together
with sigma(h) != 0 for the 7 non-zero h in span(h_5, h_6, h_7) alone, and for the 15 in
span(h_1, ..., h_4) alone. The control, with the same conditions except that T(N) has k = 10, has a
model at "n". So what is contradictory is the commuting space T(N), the hand conditions and very
little anisotropy together.

This must be read with one caution. The "conditions of the case" are not free of anisotropy: the
normal form, the hints and Lemmas 104 and 105 were derived by hand from the anisotropy of the
whole system. A hand proof can use them, since they are already proved (by hand, unchecked by a
human), but the observation does not say that anisotropy on N alone forbids such a T(N) in
general.

## The decision rules

- **Some d = 2 cases UNKNOWN:** re-run only those at 120 s in one job: job m106a, which also tries
  them with anisotropy only on N and with none. Certificates (and Proposition 39) wait until every
  d = 2 case is decided.
- **core, UNSAT at "n":** the hand conditions plus anisotropy on N already contradict; prove that
  first. **UNSAT at "noN_out3":** anisotropy on N is not needed beyond what the hand conditions
  carry. No hand proof was found in this run. Job m106e asks for the smallest sets of vectors
  inside N and inside span(h_5, h_6, h_7) that still give UNSAT, and whether no anisotropy at all
  is enough, so that a proof has a precise target.
- **k = 6, UNSAT at "out3":** the coset structure is not special to k <= 4, so nothing more is
  queued for k = 6. Job m106e only re-reads the same four cases with smaller sets.
