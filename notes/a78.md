## Limits

- The new UNSAT answers of m87a-v3 are solver answers saved without proof certificates. Corollary
  116 rests on every UNSAT answer of m86a and m87a-v3, on Proposition 33 ([a52.md](a52.md)) and
  Lemma 93, and on the reductions listed in [a68.md](a68.md). m87a-v3's grade step did not run;
  the 20 records were read one by one for this note.
- A VERIFIED answer certifies that one CNF has no solution. It does not certify that the CNF
  encodes the case it stands for: the encoding (m57.py), the hints, the unit clauses of Lemma 93 (b),
  Proposition 33's orbit roots and the reductions are code and hand arguments that no human has
  checked.
- An UNKNOWN is not evidence either way. A host stop is not a result: the 11 UNKNOWN rank-0
  instances of o003_c000 and the instances never reached count as open.
- No human mathematician has checked this corollary or any of the lemmas it uses.
