## Corollary 64 (the kind-L class of (B6) is empty)

**Corollary 64.** Let a system with t = 7 and dim V = 9 be anisotropic. Then no element of rank 3
lies in the class of (B6) with d = 3 whose Im A_u contains x0 (pair orbit 0, A_u = e_4 -> e_2,
e_5 -> e_1, e_6 -> e_0 in m42's coordinates, the mask 266304).

*Proof (computer-assisted).* By Corollary 55 there is no element of rank 4. Suppose u is such an
element. By Proposition 30 (a) ([a42.md](a42.md)), a second element h_1 of ker phi has A_(h_1) in
Adm, and up to the stabiliser of (x0, P, A_u) and h_1 -> h_1 + u, A_(h_1) is one of 308
representatives (counted by hand in Proposition 31, [a43.md](a43.md), and matched by machine in
job m59a: |G| = 12288, |Adm| = 176126, 51 / 174 / 83 by rank on X_1). With A_u and A_(h_1) fixed
and m42's hints added, each of the 308 instances is UNSAT: 301 in job m59a and a031, a115, a155,
a160, a182, a288 and a307 here. So no such u exists.

This rests on 308 solver answers saved without proof certificates, on Propositions 30 and 31 and
on the hand lemmas behind m42's hints (Lemma 88 and the earlier ones listed in [a36.md](a36.md)).
With Corollary 61 ([a47.md](a47.md)) and Corollary 62 ([a50.md](a50.md)), all three classes of
(B6) with d = 3 are empty.
