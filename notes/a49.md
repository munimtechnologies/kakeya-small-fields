- In each split, the instances of rank 2 on X_1 run first, then rank 1, then rank 0. That way the
  slow kind gets the larger share of the time left.

**o032_c000 is the first split with X_1 = span(e_0, e_1, e_2, e_6),** which is not inside H0. m57.py's
rank_on_x1 reads columns 0 to 3, which is right only when X_1 = span(e_0, ..., e_3). m59's
admissibility check used it, so m60 does not: it takes the rank on the columns at X_1's own basis
vectors. Before anything is solved, the check confirms that this rank equals m57.rank_on_x1 on
every representative of the three classes with X_1 = span(e_0, ..., e_3), and m55's own rank on
every representative of all four. It also recomputes each of the 26 re-opened instances and
compares it with the A_(h_1) that m59b ran.

Guesses (low confidence): at least 20 of the 26 UNSAT within the 5 minutes of CaDiCaL, at least 23
after both arms; o001_c000 closes; o032_c000 830 to 856 of 856; o000_c000 600 to 671 of 671.

Rules: a class all of whose instances are UNSAT is empty. A re-opened instance still open after
both arms gets the third-matrix split by hand first. A split with 1 to 30 open (SKIPPED included)
gets 5 minutes in each arm; with more than 30, a 60 s pass first.
