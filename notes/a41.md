MV2 of Munim, Inc., 2026-10-20. Added to kakeya-small-fields next to [a40.md](a40.md). This note
has no new lemmas; the next free numbers are still Lemma 93, Corollary 61, Proposition 30 and
Theorem 33. It was researched and written with AI agents working in MV2's name. **It settles no
case of A(7) <= 8: that is still open.** It grades job m46 (the 42 classes of (B5) that job m42c

## Job m50 (CryptoMiniSat with native XOR)

Every search in these notes so far solved sat_aniso.py's CNF with CaDiCaL, with each linear
condition over F_2 cut into parity clauses of at most 4 variables. These instances are mostly such
conditions: (*) and (Q) are sums of products, anisotropy is a chain of sums, and the hints are
linear. Blind budget increases have stopped paying in (B6) (m45: 5 of 30 at three times the
budget), and no class with d = 3 has ever been decided. A solver that does Gaussian elimination on
XOR constraints has never been tried here. m50 measures one before anything depends on it:

    cadical   m42.py's solver on m42.py's CNF (2 classes; a check that the host's speed matches m46)
    xor       CryptoMiniSat 5.17 (pycryptosat, default settings, one thread) with each linear
              condition as one XOR constraint (m42.build unchanged; only CNF.xor_eq differs)
    cms_cnf   CryptoMiniSat on m42.py's CNF (3 classes; separates the solver from the encoding)

    calibration  9 classes CaDiCaL decided (UNSAT): 7 of (B5) in 43 to 356 s, 2 of (B6) in 265
                 and 347 s; budget three times CaDiCaL's time, between 120 and 600 s
    targets      5 classes never decided: (B5) o000_c000 and o020_c000 (d = 3), o027_c001 (d = 1);
                 (B6) o002_c000 (d = 3), o008_c000 (d = 1); 720 s each

Graded: the classifications recomputed with the recorded representatives and the hand values of
d; on 160 random small systems and mutations, the XOR instance accepts exactly the anisotropic
systems, as the CNF does; the planted system at t = 6 is accepted and, solved in the xor arm, found
SAT and verified; t = 2 and t = 3 at dim V = t SAT and at dim V = t + 1 UNSAT; any SAT verified,
and a verified SAT on a calibration class graded as a failure (it would contradict CaDiCaL).
Guess (low confidence): no SAT; 5 to 9 of 9 calibration classes decided, with the median ratio of
CaDiCaL's time to the xor arm's time between 0.3 and 3; 0 of 5 targets. Rule: at least 7 of 9 with
a median ratio of at least 3 sends the open classes to the xor arm (first m48 b and c, as new
versions); a target with d = 3 closing would be the first d = 3 class decided, and all 13 would go
to the xor arm; otherwise the solver lever is recorded as measured and dropped.

## Where the hand work stands

No new hand fact this run. The tightness of Lemma 30 for u in (B5) (U+(u) = ker phi has dimension
4 = 1 + A(3), so ker phi / span(u) carries an anisotropic system with t = dim V = 3 in
ker A_u / span(x0)) was already noted in [a34.md](a34.md) and [a36.md](a36.md). It does not by
itself constrain that system: the trivial system (all maps 0, sigma linear and one-to-one) is
extremal for every t, so the route needs a further fact that rules the trivial quotient out.

## What does not follow

- Nothing is decided for (B6) or (B5): 14 of 68 and 350 of 420 classes are UNSAT. 7 <= A(7) <= 9.
- m46's 19 UNKNOWN answers are not evidence either way.
- The 23 new UNSAT answers are solver answers without proof certificates, like every UNSAT answer
  of jobs m42 to m46.
- m50 has not run; no speed-up is claimed.
