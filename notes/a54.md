## Job m66 (staged)

Code: cube_groups/m66.py, which imports m58.py (for the 51 class representatives) unchanged;
predictions and rules: cube_groups/m66-predictions.md, written on 2026-10-21 before it ran. For
each of the 51 classes that were open when Proposition 33 was written, it solves the linear
conditions g x0 = x0, g P = P, g A_u = A_u g for G (as job m52 did, not from the parametrisation
of Proposition 33 (b)) and counts the classes of Adm under G and A -> A + A_u by union-find.
Conjugation is linear in A, so each generator acts on the 18-bit index of A through three tables
of 64 entries, checked against direct conjugation on 200 maps per class. Graded: every |G| of
Proposition 33 (c), |Adm| of (a), the 7 exact counts of (d), and orbit sizes that divide 2 |G| and
add up to |Adm|. The counts for d >= 1, which were not done by hand, are reported with a guess of 2
to 8 times their lower bound. The orbit roots (the smallest index in each orbit, so they do not
depend on the generators) are the split instances for the classes that stay open. The job runs no
solver and decides no class.
