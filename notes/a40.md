## Job m49 (Corollary 60 as clauses)

In m42.py's normal form, N = ker phi = span(h_0 = u, h_1, h_2) in (B6), A_u is fixed and h -> A_h is
linear. Corollary 60 asks A_n != 0 for each of the 6 elements n = e u + sum_(i in S) h_i with S a
non-empty subset of {1, 2} and e in {0, 1}: one clause each over the entries of
e A_u + sum_(i in S) A_(h_i), with 49 auxiliary variables (the entries of A_(h_1) + A_(h_2), each
an XOR of two variables) shared by the two clauses with S = {1, 2}. In (B5), kd = 4 gives 14
clauses and 196 auxiliary variables. Nothing else in the instance changes.

    m49a   the 13 classes m45b left UNKNOWN with d >= 1 (all but o011_c004), 624 s each
    m49b   the 10 classes m45a left UNKNOWN with d >= 1 (all but o012_c001), 624 s each

Graded (each must hold): m45.py's check of the classification; 6 clauses and 49 auxiliary
variables on a real instance and 14 and 196 for kd = 4 (counted by hand); on 400 random and
constructed choices of the maps A_(h_i), the clauses hold exactly when every A_n != 0, and both
outcomes occur; the planted system at t = 6 (every A_n != 0 on its N) is accepted by the CNF with
the clauses and found SAT and verified (a positive control that the clauses cut no true model);
any SAT at t = 7 verified.

Guess (low confidence): no SAT; 0 to 3 of 13 in m49a and 0 to 3 of 10 in m49b UNSAT; the d = 3
classes stay UNKNOWN. Rule: if m49a and m49b together close at least 7 of the 23, Corollary 60
helps the solver, and the same clauses go to the 24 classes of m42b's half with d >= 1 and to the
open (B5) classes with d >= 2. With fewer, the clause route is dropped as a lever and the hand
route comes first; one or two closures are not read as a speed-up, since any change to the CNF
changes the solver's search. Every class m49 closes is UNSAT given Corollary 60, so it counts for
(B6) on top of Lemmas 89 to 92 (by hand).
