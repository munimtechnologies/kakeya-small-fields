## Proposition 39

**Proposition 39 (computer-assisted).** Let (A, sigma, beta) be an anisotropic system with t = 8
and dim V = 11 in the rank-0 case of (B7)_8, in m42's normal form, and let d = dim(X_1 n R) for its
pair orbit ([a97.md](a97.md)). If d >= 2, then the commuting space T(N) leaves at least 6
subspaces of Q invariant.

*Proof.* Lemma 106 ([a94.md](a94.md)) splits the rank-0 case of each class into the instances of
job m102: t1, and one t2 or t3 case per orbit of Kbar on the subspaces T(N) can be. For d = 3 the
cases with k(T(N)) <= 4 are the 57 of Proposition 38. For d = 2 they are the 1,115 cases of jobs
m108a-d (job m104a's count, by class). For each, the CNF that was certified is implied by the
conditions of the case: m102's sweep CNF encodes the case with anisotropy on all of V, and the CNF
used for four of them keeps every condition but asks anisotropy only on the 15 non-zero vectors of
N. drat-trim verified a refutation of each. So no system has such a case. ∎

What this rests on: the hand lemmas that set up the cases (Lemmas 102 to 106 and Corollary 123,
[a92.md](a92.md) to [a94.md](a94.md)), the count k(T(N)) of m103 and m104 (Lemma 108,
[a95.md](a95.md), checked by brute force in [a96.md](a96.md)),
and the encoders m42, m100, m101 and m102, which no human has checked. A DRAT proof certifies the
CNF only, not that the CNF says what the note says it does.

In numbers: of the 6,548 cases with k <= 4 in the 68 classes of the rank-0 case of (B7)_8, 1,172
(d = 2 and d = 3) are now ruled out with checked certificates; 3,876 (d = 1) and 1,500 (d = 0)
remain, beside the 9,656 cases with k >= 6 and the 68 t1 cases.

## The decision rules

- **No verified model anisotropic on all of V** in any job: nothing to re-check.
- **m108a-d, all 1,115 VERIFIED:** Proposition 39, and next the d = 1 cases with k <= 4 (job m110).
- **m109, no small system** (the smallest keeps 8 indices, 6 rows and 798 equations): the
  small-system rule does not fire. The other rule asked for 9 or more indices in every item; two
  items keep 8, so it does not fire to the letter, but its reading holds: the contradiction is
  spread over V and a hand proof along this route is not promising. Lemma 110 is set aside; the
  d = 1 cases come next.
