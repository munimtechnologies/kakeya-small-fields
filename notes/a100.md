**Observation (uncertified).** For each of these ten commuting spaces T(N) there is no solution of
the system (*) on V = F_2^11 with m42's normal form and A_(v_2), A_(v_3) equal to A_u-bar T pi for

normal form, the reduction to the rank-0 case and the form of A_(v_2), A_(v_3) (Lemma 106) were

general. It means that a hand proof of these cases needs only (*), the normal form and M. The
positive control (k = 10, M = J) is SAT with the same twelve groups, so M does not contradict (*)
for every T. Job m108g/h asks which equations of (*) the contradiction uses.

## The decision rules

- **m107a, all four UNSAT:** DRAT certificates of all 1,115 d = 2 cases, each from the CNF that was
  UNSAT for it (m102's sweep CNF, or for these four the CNF with anisotropy on N): job m108a-d,
  four shards of 279, 279, 279 and 278 cases (job m104 needed about 36 s per case).
- **m107b + m107c, not all 88 UNSAT:** many cases are SAT at both levels, so "none" is not scaled to
  the k > 4 cases of the d = 2 classes. The 21 UNSAT are not certified yet. The results are reported
  by k and type above.
- **m107e + m107f, one small kept set shared by all ten:** {M} is the target of a hand proof
  (Lemma 110: (*), the normal form and M contradict each other for that T(N)). Before writing it,
  job m108g/h splits (*) into 16 groups, (L) A_j s_i = b(i, j) + A_i b(i, j) by the kinds (u, v,
  h) of i and j, and (Q) A_j b(i, k) + A_i b(k, j) + A_k b(i, j) = 0 by the kinds of {i, j, k}, and
  finds a deletion-minimal set of them for each of the ten cases. m108h also runs "M only" on the
  twelve other k = 6 cases that were UNSAT at "none".
