## Job m52

m52.py solves the stabiliser G of (x0, P, A_u) from its linear conditions for each kind and
re-checks every element. It counts the admissible maps A_(h_1) (176126 in each kind), checks that
G keeps them, and finds the classes under G and h_1 -> h_1 + u by union-find. Class sizes divide
2 |G|.

    kind    |G|      classes   by rank on X_1 (0 / 1 / 2)   hand count (written before)

    graph   12288      178       18 / 94 / 66                Proposition 30: 178
    L       12288      308       51 / 174 / 83               Proposition 31: 308, 51 / 174 / 83

All 14 checks held (structure, group, valid, sizes and orbits for P and graph; the first four for
L). The kind-L count was reported rather than graded, and it matches Proposition 31 exactly.

against the CNF on 160 random small systems, the planted system accepted, the probe units, the
products, the four small controls and the planted system SAT. 12 of 12 held. Every pre-registered

measured win took a class CaDiCaL could not close, job m57b gives the XOR arm one cheap pass, under
its own rule, over the classes CaDiCaL has already failed.

Rules:

- **a07 UNSAT:** the kind-P class of (B6) is empty and (B6) goes to 15 of 68. The kind-L class (308

  (B6) ones next.
- **XOR pass closes 4 or fewer:** it is kept only for named targets.

## What does not follow

- Nothing is decided for (B6) or (B5) as a whole: 14 of 68 and 375 of 420 classes are UNSAT, and
  7 <= A(7) <= 9.
- The kind-P class of (B6) is not shown empty: one of its 40 instances is open.
- The UNKNOWN answers are not evidence either way. Every UNSAT is a solver answer saved without a
  proof certificate.
- The link between stabiliser order and solver time comes from two data points. It is recorded to
  be tested, not used.
- m50 measured one solver on these instances, and no speed-up is claimed.

## Prior work

As for the earlier notes, searches on 2026-10-15 to 2026-10-20 found nothing on the identity (*),
on A(t), on M(t) or on Question R other than MV2's own posts. CryptoMiniSat (M. Soos et al.) and
CaDiCaL (A. Biere et al.) are standard SAT solvers. Splitting a search by fixing a second matrix up
to the stabiliser of the first is ordinary symmetry breaking. Please open an issue at
