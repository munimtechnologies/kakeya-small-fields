## Setting (from Lemma 85)

X = F_2^7 with basis e_0, ..., e_6; W = ker A_u = span(e_0, e_1, e_2); Y = span(e_1, e_2) = W n P;
P = span(e_1, ..., e_6); R = Im A_u of dimension 4 with dim(R n W) >= 2 (Corollary 51);

    K = {g in GL(X) : g e_0 = e_0, g W = W, g P = P},   |K| = 6 x 2^8 x 20160 = 30965760,

in blocks on P (basis e_1, e_2 | e_3, ..., e_6): g = [b M; 0 A] with b in GL_2, A in GL_4, M any
2 x 4 matrix. By Lemma 85 the system can be changed by any g in K, so R is one of five
representatives and A_u is fixed up to conjugation by Stab_K(R) = {g in K : g R = R}.

Write Q = X / W, with basis [e_3], ..., [e_6], so that the induced map Abar of A on Q is A itself.
Write R_W = R n W, r = dim R_W in {2, 3}, Rbar = (R + W) / W, a subspace of Q of dimension 4 - r.
A_u with kernel W and image R is the same thing as an isomorphism L : Q -> R (A_u x = L[x]), and
conjugation by g in Stab_K(R) replaces L by

    L' = g|_R . L . A^(-1).                                                       (C)

Let pi : R -> R / R_W be the quotient map. The natural map R -> (R + W) / W identifies R / R_W with
Rbar, so pi(x) = [x] in Q, and for g in Stab_K(R)

    pi . g|_R = A . pi    on R.                                                   (P)

Set T = pi . L : Q -> Q. It is an endomorphism of Q with image Rbar and kernel Z = L^(-1)(R_W),
of rank 4 - r. By (C) and (P), T' = A T A^(-1): **T changes by conjugation under the image of
Stab_K(R) in GL(Q)**, and that image is the stabiliser of Rbar in GL_4 (types YP, X, G), the
subgroup of it that fixes a linear form on Rbar (type Yn), or the stabiliser of the vector [e_3]
(type W). Everything below counts orbits of pairs (T, extra data).

## Lemma 86 (the classes of A_u in shape (c))

**Lemma 86.** Let t = 7, dim V = 9, u of rank 4 in Case B, shape (c), in the form of Lemma 37, and
let R = Im A_u be one of the five representatives of Lemma 85. The classes of A_u (the 20160
matrices with kernel W and image R) under conjugation by Stab_K(R), and their sizes, are:

    type   |Stab_K(R)|   classes   sizes
    W      2064384       5         1344, 4032, 4032, 5376, 5376
    YP     884736        6         1536, 4608, 3072, 6912, 3456, 576
    Yn     294912        10        1536; 1536, 3072; 3072; 2304, 2304, 2304; 1152, 2304; 576
    X      73728         18        384, 1152; 1152, 1152, 2304; 768, 2304; 1152 (six times);
                                   1152, 576, 576, 1152; 576
    G      73728         15        384, 1152; 1152, 1152, 2304; 768, 2304; 2304, 2304, 1152, 1152;
                                   2304, 576, 576; 576

54 classes in all. In each row the sizes add up to 20160 and each divides |Stab_K(R)|. The
semicolons group the classes by the similarity class of T (below). With the same conventions, at
t = 6 (A_u of rank 3, X = F_2^6) the counts are W 1, YP 2, Yn 2, X 5, G 4 (14 in all), and at t = 5
(rank 2) YP 1, X 1, G 1 (3 in all).

*Proof.* *Step 1 (type W, as in a29.md, with sizes).* R = W + span(e_3), r = 3, Rbar = [e_3]. The
stabiliser is b, M free and A e_3 = e_3 (|Stab(e_3)| = 20160 / 15 = 1344), so |Stab_K(R)| =
6 x 256 x 1344 = 2064384. The map L -> L[e_3] sends the 20160 isomorphisms onto the 15 non-zero
vectors of R, with fibres of size 1344 (two L with the same value differ by an element of
Stab([e_3]) on the right), and the right action of A runs through each fibre. The left action is by
g|_R, which fixes e_0, acts on Y by b and sends e_3 to e_3 + y, y in Y: 24 maps, independent of A.
Their orbits on the non-zero vectors of R have sizes 1 (e_0), 3 (Y \ 0), 3 (e_0 + Y \ 0), 4 (e_3 + Y),
4 (e_0 + e_3 + Y). So 5 classes of sizes 1344, 4032, 4032, 5376, 5376.

*Step 2 (the similarity class of T, for r = 2).* Here T has rank 2 and image Rbar. If A T A^(-1) = T'
and both have image Rbar, then A Rbar = Rbar. So the orbits of Stab(Rbar) on the endomorphisms of
Q with image Rbar are exactly the GL_4(F_2)-similarity classes of endomorphisms of rank 2. Rank 2
means nullity 2, so the nilpotent part of T (on the generalised kernel, of dimension d) has exactly
two Jordan blocks, and the rest is an invertible map on a complement of dimension 4 - d. The classes:

    T1 = 0_2 (+) I_2                centraliser order 36      class size 560     with image Rbar: 16
    T2 = 0_2 (+) J,  J = [1 1; 0 1]                    12                 1680                     48
    T3 = 0_2 (+) S,  S of order 3 (x^2 + x + 1)        18                 1120                     32
    T4 = J_2(0) (+) J_1(0) (+) (1)                      8                 2520                     72
    T5 = J_3(0) (+) J_1(0)                             16                 1260                     36
    T6 = J_2(0) (+) J_2(0)                             96                  210                      6

The class sizes add up to 7350 = (15 x 14)^2 / (3 x 2), the number of 4 x 4 matrices of rank 2 over
F_2, and the last column is the class size divided by 35 (GL_4 is transitive on planes), adding up to
210 = 15 x 14, the number of surjections F_2^4 -> F_2^2. The centraliser orders: for T1, T2, T3 it is
|GL_2| times the centraliser of the invertible part in GL_2 (6, 2, 3); for a nilpotent T with
partition lambda the centraliser in GL_n(F_2) has order 2^(dim C - sum m_i^2) prod |GL_(m_i)(F_2)|,
with dim C = sum of the squares of the parts of the conjugate partition and m_i the multiplicities
of the parts: (2, 1) in GL_3 gives 2^(5 - 2) = 8; (3, 1) gives 2^(6 - 2) = 16; (2, 2) gives
2^(8 - 4) x 6 = 96.

Below, Z = ker T and C(T) is the centraliser of T in GL_4; C(T) lies in Stab(Rbar). Explicit bases:
for T1, T2, T3, Q = Z (+) E with E = Im T, and C(T) = GL(Z) x C_(GL(E))(T|_E). For T4: T a_2 = a_1,
T a_1 = T a_3 = 0, T a_4 = a_4; Z = span(a_1, a_3); C(T) is a_1 -> a_1, a_2 -> a_2 + beta a_1 +
gamma a_3, a_3 -> a_3 + delta a_1, a_4 -> a_4 (8 elements). For T5: T a_3 = a_2, T a_2 = a_1,
T a_1 = T a_4 = 0; Z = span(a_1, a_4); C(T) is a_1 -> a_1, a_2 -> a_2 + beta a_1, a_3 -> a_3 +
beta a_2 + gamma a_1 + eps a_4, a_4 -> a_4 + delta a_1 (16 elements). For T6: T a_2 = a_1, T a_4 =
a_3, Z = Im T = span(a_1, a_3); C(T) consists of the g with g a_2, g a_4 arbitrary and independent
modulo Z (96 elements), it induces all of GL(Q / Z) and, through T, the same matrices on Z.

*Step 3 (what is left when T is fixed).* Fix T with image Rbar and kernel Z. The isomorphisms L with
pi L = T are the maps L_0 + (any map Q -> R_W) that are invertible, and L is invertible exactly when
L|_Z : Z -> R_W is (T identifies Q / Z with Rbar). So there are 6 x 16 = 96 of them, and
210 x 96 = 20160 as it must be. Choose a complement R_1 of R_W in R mapped by pi onto Rbar and
write rho for the projection R -> R_W along R_1. The elements of Stab_K(R) with A = I act on R as
g|_R = [c m; 0 I] in R = R_W (+) R_1, with c = g|_(R_W) and m : R_1 -> R_W, and they replace
rho L by c . rho L + m . T. The term m . T is a map Q -> R_W that vanishes on Z and whose values lie
in Im m; off Z it is free within Im m.

- Types YP and Yn: R_W = Y, c = b runs over all of GL(Y), and m runs over all of Hom(R_1, Y)
  (M is free: for YP, R = Y + span(e_3, e_4); for Yn, R = Y + span(e_0 + e_3, e_4), and
  g(e_0 + e_3) = e_0 + e_3 + M e_3 stays in R for every M). So b moves L|_Z to any isomorphism
  Z -> Y and m . T moves rho L off Z freely: **the 96 maps with a given T form one class.** Hence the
  classes of A_u are the orbits of the image of Stab_K(R) in GL(Q) on the T with image Rbar.
- Type X: R = span(e_0, e_1, e_3, e_4), R_W = span(e_0, e_1). g R = R forces b e_1 = e_1 and
  M e_3, M e_4 in span(e_1), so c is the identity on R_W and Im m = span(e_1). Set lambda =

  (lambda|_Z, mu) is a basis of Z^*: lambda is one of the 12 forms not vanishing on Z and mu one of
  the 2 forms outside span(lambda|_Z). The A = I elements change only e_1^* . L off Z (by m . T),

  of size 4 for each T. By (C) and (P), A acts by lambda -> lambda . A^(-1) and mu -> mu . A^(-1)

  of GL_4 on triples (T, lambda, mu), or, for a fixed T, of C(T) on the 24 pairs. Write

  as (l', l_Z, H_0) with H_0 = ker lambda a hyperplane through l_Z not containing Z (4 such

- Type G: R = span(e_1, e_0 + e_2, e_3, e_4), R_W = span(e_1, e_0 + e_2). Now b e_1 = e_1 and
  b e_2 in e_2 + span(e_1), so c is the identity or the transvection e_1 -> e_1, e_0 + e_2 ->
  e_0 + e_2 + e_1 of R_W; Im m = span(e_1) as for type X. In the coordinates (x, y) of R_W with
  respect to (e_1, e_0 + e_2), the transvection is (x, y) -> (x + y, y). With lambda = the
  y-coordinate of L (in Q^*, not vanishing on Z) and mu = the x-coordinate of L|_Z, the transvection
  replaces mu by mu + lambda|_Z and so identifies the two admissible mu. The invariant is lambda

  lambda in Q^* with lambda|_Z != 0.

  (A e_3 in e_3 + span(e_4), A e_4 = e_4). Two T, T' with image Rbar are in one class iff

  GL_4-conjugate, where l_0 = ker f = [e_4] is a line in Rbar. So the classes are the orbits of

## How job m44 checks this

m44.py writes its selfcheck to out/selfcheck.json and the classes to out/classes_t5.json,
classes_t6.json and classes_t7.json. Lemma 86 is graded by:

1. At t = 5 and t = 6 the classes are exact (every element of K is listed): `exact["5"]["classes"]`
   must be 3 and `exact["6"]["classes"]` must be 14. A different number means Lemma 86 is wrong at
   that t (the note is then corrected; m44's solver answers are unaffected).
2. At t = 7 (`split["per_r"]` with `split["types"]`): the counts by type must be W 5, YP 6, Yn 10,
   X 18, G 15. The machine classes are closures under 24 random units, so the machine count is
   **at least** the true one and equal to it when the units generate the stabiliser; a machine count
   below the hand count means Lemma 86 is wrong; a count above it with `stable` true is a warning to
   look at the sizes: a true class has one of the sizes in the table, a split one a proper divisor.
3. The class sizes in classes_t7.json, by orbit of R, must be the multisets in the table (same rule
   on splitting).
4. (Recorded) 54 classes at t = 7 means m44b, m44c, m44d run 13 or 14 classes each with about
   10 minutes per class.

## What does not follow

- Nothing about A(7) <= 8: Lemma 86 counts instances; it decides none.
- Lemma 86 is by hand and unchecked by machine until m44 reports. Its sizes and totals pass the
  internal checks of Step 5, and the t = 5 and t = 6 counts follow the method that gave machine-
  matched counts in Lemmas 80, 82, 84 and 85, but a wrong orbit count would not show in those sums.
